Where should RoamWise pay you?

For a booking you accepted. RoamWise buys the stay from you at your agreed rate and pays you directly, the same day it receives these details. They are used only to pay you.

Please bill RoamWise Technologies, Almora, Uttarakhand (GSTIN on your booking email). If your details change later, RoamWise will call you on your known number before paying to the new account.
