Questions

How do I book a stay in Almora through RoamWise?

Pick a stay, then message the property on WhatsApp from RoamWise. Your message carries a short RoamWise code such as RW-7KQ2MX, so the host knows you found them here. Rooms, dates and the final total are confirmed directly with the property.

Do I pay RoamWise?

For stays, follow the property's own payment instructions and pay the host directly. RoamWise does not hold your stay payment.

What is the RW code for?

It is a short reference added to your enquiry. It lets you confirm later that you stayed, and only your own browser can do that. It stores no name, phone number or email.
