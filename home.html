Start with the decision that costs money

Before choosing attractions, identify the booking that controls the trip: the stay location, a scarce connection, an activity with strict terms or the final kilometres of transport. Compare complete costs with the same dates, inclusions, taxes and cancellation rights. An attractive headline price may describe a different product.

The travel-budget worksheet shows how to label quotes, estimates and actual spending. The whole-journey comparison counts travel to and from stations, waiting and check-in time. The Tatkal guide explains the train-origin date and current identity requirements without promising a seat.

Use the planner as a draft

The RoamWise app can organise an itinerary, compare themes and build a budget. Treat its result as a starting plan. Verify live transport, prices, opening conditions, entry rules and safety information with the responsible provider before booking. Save essential confirmations offline and keep one optional activity removable.

How to judge these articles

Each page includes the source URL, the limited claim it supports and the date checked. Illustrative prices are arithmetic inputs, never presented as current market research. Suggestions explain whose needs they fit. If you find a mistake, the editorial policy explains how to send evidence and how corrections are handled.
