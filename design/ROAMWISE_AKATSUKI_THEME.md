# RoamWise Akatsuki inspired design template

An original night-sky visual layer inspired by crimson cloud motifs and cinematic shadow palettes. It keeps RoamWise’s Himalayan travel imagery, gold wayfinding, violet accents and readable product UI. It uses no Naruto characters, insignia or copied artwork.

## Palette

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night ink | #080910 | Deep page and panel base |
| Crimson | #C4303C | Cloud glints, selected states and small emphasis |
| Bright crimson | #E54B5A | Focus accents and decorative strokes |
| Himalayan violet | #875CE3 | AI and discovery glow |
| RoamWise gold | #E8BA6C | Primary actions, borders and navigation |

## Rules

Use soft rounded panels, a fine gold edge, restrained crimson glow and one abstract cloud line. Keep controls at least 42px on phones, preserve focus rings and honor reduced-motion settings. Red decoration stays away from body text and essential controls.

The reusable layer is `design/roamwise-akatsuki-theme.css`; `index.html` applies it and `sw.js` includes it in the offline shell. Phone composer sizing remains independently adjustable in `mobile-stability.css`.

Keep real destination photography and established RoamWise gold/violet styling. Use crimson for emphasis and alerts, not as the general text color.
