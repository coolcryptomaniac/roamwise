What to include

  • App problems: your device, whether you used the app or the website, and what you tapped just before it went wrong. A screenshot solves most reports instantly.
  • Wrong information: tell me the place and what is out of date. Cost bands and crowd patterns shift, and corrections from people who were actually there are the most valuable thing I receive.
  • Payments and Pro: the UPI reference or transaction ID, and the email on the account.
  • Creator partnerships: use the creator page — it captures what I need to reply properly.
  • Data requests: to see, correct or delete what is held about you, email from the address on your account and say what you want. See the privacy policy.

Reporting abuse

If someone on RoamWise has attempted a scam, harassed you, or behaved in a way that made you uncomfortable, report it in the app or email me directly. Scam attempts and harassment result in a permanent ban enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the interface.

One honest limit: I can remove an account. I cannot recover money or pursue a person. If you have been defrauded or threatened, report it to cybercrime.gov.in as well, or call the national cyber helpline on 1930.
