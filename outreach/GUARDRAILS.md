# Outreach guardrails (India)

Autopilot only does what these rules allow. Anything outside them is done by the founder, one message at a time.

- **Email only, one person at a time.** No bulk send, no CC/BCC. At most 3 a day, nearest city first.
- **Public business contact only.** Use an address the creator or business publishes for work. Never scrape, buy or guess addresses.
- **Personal and relevant.** Each email names something real the creator made. If none can be named, do not send.
- **Easy opt-out.** Every email says "reply no and I won't write again". A "no", unsubscribe or complaint stops all further mail to that person. Record it in outreach/creators.json as status "declined".
- **No automatic WhatsApp to people who have not asked.** Share the link only in groups or chats the founder is already part of, or after the person replies. WhatsApp's own rules and TRAI commercial-message rules apply.
- **Respect the DPDP Act 2023.** Collect only what registration needs, keep sign-up consent explicit (the form already requires it), and delete a person's data on request.
- **Courtesy.** No pressure, no fake urgency, no claims about reach or earnings. Terms are stated as agreed with each property and as subject to change.
- **No money moves automatically.** Properties pay performance cash after a completed stay. RoamWise does not hold or send it.
- **Tracking is aggregate.** The hub shows counts by source, stage and day. It does not log what people read or click.
