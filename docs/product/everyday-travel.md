# Everyday travel needs

## Positioning
Prioritize repeat-use travel services where local coordination, accurate information and support matter. No category has guaranteed constant demand, and adding a planning entry point does not establish supply or profitability.

| Need | Budget offer to source | Comfort offer to source | Premium offer to source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly / relocation stay | Basic private room or shared accommodation | Furnished room with kitchen access and reliable Wi-Fi | Private serviced space with agreed housekeeping |
| Work / exams / interviews | Stay close to venue; public transport guidance | Quiet room and scheduled transfer | Flexible private stay and dedicated transfer |
| Family visits | Simple family room | Kitchen access, suitable room layout | Private multi-room accommodation |
| Hospital-adjacent stays | Attendant lodging near the facility | Accessible room, kitchen and ordinary transport | Private accommodation with agreed support services |
| Essential transfers | Public/shared option where available | Scheduled taxi; EV preference where suitable | Dedicated vehicle with agreed waiting allowance |
| Pilgrimage logistics | Basic stay and shared transport | Family stay with local coordination | Private stay and dedicated guide/transport |

These are supply-development targets, not advertised live inventory. Hospital-adjacent planning covers lodging and ordinary transport, not healthcare or emergency transport. Never equate premium with guaranteed safety or accessibility.

## Commercial approach
- Use existing agreed partner commissions; avoid funding customer discounts from Roamwise's margin.
- Quote any future coordination/service fee explicitly before approval; this change introduces no fee.
- Separate essentials from optional extras. Offer repeat booking convenience instead of unconditional cashback.
- Source partners around one local corridor first, with written rates, cancellation terms, availability contacts and a backup provider.
- Verify Wi-Fi, stairs/lifts, kitchen access, pickup coverage and EV suitability with the provider. Do not infer them from a property name or price tier.
- Scale only after completed bookings show positive contribution: collected revenue minus payment fees, support effort, refunds and subsidies. Review repeat bookings and provider response time.

## Delivered interface
The homepage has a collapsible planner for six needs, three preferences and an explicit whole-request INR budget. It prepares a prompt in the existing Ailon Tusk input for the user to review and send. It does not overwrite an unsent draft, persist the form, book, charge, or claim verified supply. Dates and group size are requested by the prompt.

`everyday_request_prepared` is an anonymous intent counter only. Do not treat it as a confirmed lead, reservation or revenue. No destination, budget or hospital details are included in that event.

## Evidence and limits
India's Ministry of Tourism reports a substantial domestic travel market and has a medical/wellness tourism strategy: https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=281995 . This supports exploring related needs; it does not prove year-round demand in Roamwise's destinations. Validate local demand and economics through completed paid requests.
