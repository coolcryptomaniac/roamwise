RoamWise · everyday utility

Make room for what matters.

Keep essential costs in view, compare the journeys you must make and log small expenses. Made for ordinary weeks, not just holidays. All amounts are entered by you—there are no invented fares or live-price claims.

Free · no sign-inStored on this deviceNo booking or payment
Your entries stay in this browser unless you choose to open a route in Google Maps. Avoid entering an exact home address on a shared device.

Monthly essentials compass

Enter your own planned monthly amounts. Leave a category blank if it doesn't apply. Include commuting within transport, or leave transport blank; these totals are deliberately not added together twice.

Necessary journey desk

For work, caregiving, appointments or regular errands. Compare only costs you know; these are monthly estimates, not transport quotes or route recommendations.

    Maps opens only if you tap a button. Its availability and fares depend on your city. Verify routes and current conditions yourself.

    Daily essential spending log

    Keep a simple record of spending. The logged total is separate from your planned monthly amounts; entering an expense does not initiate a transaction.

      Your privacy comes first

      Data is kept only in this browser's local storage; it is not synced to your RoamWise account. Clearing browser data, switching devices or private mode can erase it. Don't enter account numbers, medication names or precise home addresses. Open Maps only when you choose to send route endpoints to Google.
