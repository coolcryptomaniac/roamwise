Property Partnership & Guest-Value MOU

Between RoamWise and the participating property, . Authorised representative: property owner or authorised manager.

Core principle: RoamWise aims to lower distribution friction while preserving sustainable margins for both parties. Commercial terms may be negotiated property by property, but guest-facing prices must remain transparent and must not be artificially inflated merely to absorb platform charges.

Agreed terms

1. Purpose and non-exclusivity. The property remains independently owned/managed and may sell directly or through other platforms. This MOU does not require exclusivity.
2. Listing contribution and negotiation. RoamWise normally applies a one-time listing contribution, with ₹299 as the current base anchor for a small property. The actual amount may be negotiated case by case and recorded below. Any discount or waiver is commercial discretion, not a permanent entitlement. Paying more does not purchase ranking or a better review outcome.
3. Mutually exclusive booking economics. Each rate plan uses either (a) a completed-booking commission model or (b) a protected confidential net-rate model under which RoamWise earns the difference between the agreed net amount and guest sell price. RoamWise will not charge both commission and a net-rate markup on the same booking unless a later written schedule expressly replaces this rule.
4. Completed-booking commission. Under the commission model, the default pilot rate is 8% of Commissionable Booking Value unless the property-specific schedule states another percentage. Commission is earned only on a completed/chargeable stay, subject to refunds, cancellations, no-shows and dispute outcomes.
5. Guest-facing platform/payment fees. RoamWise may charge a clearly disclosed booking, service or payment fee where commercially and legally appropriate. Any such amount must be visible before payment and included in the all-in price comparison. Hidden mandatory platform charges are not permitted.
6. All-in price integrity. RoamWise may compare the final guest payable amount against current public channels using the same dates, occupancy, room/rate plan, meals, cancellation flexibility, mandatory property fees and statutory taxes. Bank-funded/card-funded coupons and loyalty benefits should be identified separately. A cheaper competitor rate creates a review item, not an automatic breach.
7. No destructive price war. RoamWise aims to offer strong guest value but is not required to sell below cost or sacrifice a sustainable contribution margin merely to be the absolute cheapest. If a competitor temporarily undercuts the viable RoamWise price, the parties may improve the supplier rate, pause instant booking, use request-to-book, or add a genuine documented guest benefit.
8. Guest-value pass-through. A property may not raise its RoamWise base/net rate solely to neutralise RoamWise's lower distribution cost. Where commercially possible, a meaningful part of the distribution saving should benefit the guest through a lower final payable amount or a clearly disclosed equivalent benefit.
9. Taxes, GST/VAT and invoices. Each party is responsible for taxes applicable to its own supplies and role. Before activation the property must provide its legal name, tax-registration status and identifiers required for lawful invoicing. The booking engine should determine, by jurisdiction and transaction, whether accommodation tax is invoiced/remitted by the property, RoamWise as marketplace/deemed supplier, or another payment/remittance party. RoamWise separately accounts for tax on its own listing, commission, subscription and service-fee supplies where applicable.
10. India marketplace compliance. For Indian properties, RoamWise may need to apply GST marketplace rules, statutory TCS and/or income-tax withholding depending on the supplier's registration status, payment flow and applicable law. Such statutory collections/withholdings are shown separately in settlement statements and are not disguised as additional commission. If law changes, the current statutory treatment supersedes any outdated example in this MOU.
11. Global tax flexibility. Outside India, the parties acknowledge that VAT/GST/sales tax, occupancy/city/tourism taxes, withholding and marketplace deemed-supplier/remittance rules vary by country and locality. RoamWise may update jurisdiction-specific tax profiles without renegotiating the commercial percentage, provided the guest-facing checkout and partner settlement remain transparent.
12. Settlement statement. Each partner reconciliation should separately show accommodation/package value, statutory taxes, RoamWise commission or net-rate economics, RoamWise service/payment fees if any, refunds/adjustments, statutory withholding/TCS/TDS if applicable, and net amount payable to or receivable from the property.
13. Availability and booking confirmation. Until live inventory is technically and contractually enabled, a request is not confirmed until the property confirms availability and the final room, total payable, cancellation policy and mandatory charges.
14. Accurate content. Names, location, room type, occupancy, amenities, access, policies and photographs must materially represent what the guest receives. Materially misleading or unrelated photos, hidden mandatory charges and bait pricing are prohibited.
15. Content licence. The property grants RoamWise a non-exclusive licence during the relationship to host, crop, resize and display approved photographs, videos, logos, descriptions and factual property information in the website, app, itinerary/PDF outputs, booking pages and RoamWise marketing/social material. Ownership remains with the original rights holder.
16. Quality and guest experience. Repeated material complaints involving safety, cleanliness, misrepresentation, undisclosed charges, refusal to honour accepted terms or poor communication may lead to pause, ranking reduction or delisting after review.
17. Refunds, cancellation and disputes. Property cancellation terms must be shown before payment. Commission/settlement may be reversed or adjusted to reflect genuine refunds, chargebacks and accepted disputes. RoamWise may hold disputed settlement amounts while a case is investigated where lawful.
18. Compliance and licences. The property remains responsible for lawful operation, registrations, licences, guest-ID obligations, local tourism rules and taxes applicable to its accommodation. RoamWise remains responsible for obligations applicable to its own platform/intermediary/payment role.
19. Data and confidentiality. Non-public net rates, commercial schedules and personal data must be used only for legitimate partnership/booking purposes and handled under applicable law. Public forms must not collect banking passwords, gateway secrets or card credentials.
20. Termination and survival. Either party may end future distribution on reasonable notice. RoamWise may immediately pause new bookings for credible safety, fraud, material misrepresentation or regulatory concerns. Existing guest, refund, tax, dispute and settlement obligations survive as needed.
21. Pilot framework; future definitive agreement. This MOU records the commercial pilot. Property-specific schedules, payment-provider contracts, statutory requirements or a later signed definitive agreement may supplement or supersede it.
22. Governing framework. For an India-based property under this version, Indian law is the intended governing framework subject to mandatory local rights and jurisdiction. International properties should receive the jurisdiction-specific addendum before live booking.
