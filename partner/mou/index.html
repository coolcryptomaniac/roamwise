Property Partnership & Guest-Value MOU

Between RoamWise / GYANVERSE and the participating property, . Contact/authorised representative: property owner or authorised manager.

Core principle: RoamWise's lower distribution cost must create a real customer benefit. A property may not inflate its RoamWise rate merely to absorb the lower platform commission.

Agreed terms

1. Purpose and non-exclusivity. The parties may test distribution of verified accommodation through RoamWise. The property remains independently owned/managed, controls lawful inventory and may sell through its own channels or other platforms. Nothing here prevents the property from offering lower rates elsewhere.
2. Commission. RoamWise earns the applicable commission only on qualifying completed bookings attributed to RoamWise, subject to refunds, reversals, disputes and the agreed settlement process. Taxes, payment-gateway charges and optional paid promotion, if any, must be separately disclosed and may not be hidden inside an invented room-rate markup.
3. Guest-value pass-through. The property will set its RoamWise rate from a genuine property/base rate and will not increase that rate solely to offset RoamWise's lower commission. Where the property has verified evidence that another comparable distribution channel charges a materially higher supplier commission than RoamWise, the property will pass a meaningful part of that distribution-cost saving to the guest. The preferred form is a lower like-for-like RoamWise guest price; where a documented contractual, tax or channel restriction prevents that, RoamWise may approve a clearly stated equivalent benefit such as breakfast, upgrade, transfer, flexible cancellation or late checkout.
4. Pricing-integrity audit. Before activation, RoamWise may compare the property's proposed rate against at least two current public sources/OTAs for the same dates, occupancy, room class, meal inclusion, cancellation terms and mandatory fees. A cheaper public offer does not automatically create a breach; it creates a review item. Material unexplained overpricing, hidden mandatory charges, bait pricing or an intentional markup designed to neutralise RoamWise's lower commission must be corrected before the listing is approved.
5. Commission-differential check. If the property supplies reliable evidence of an OTA's actual supplier commission and that verified percentage exceeds the RoamWise percentage, the parties may use the same-net reference calculation as an audit target: comparator guest rate × (1 − comparator commission) ÷ (1 − RoamWise commission). This is a transparency tool, not a restriction on the price the property may offer on another platform.
6. Accurate content and current photography. The property warrants that names, map location, room type, capacity, amenities, views, access, policies and photographs supplied to RoamWise materially represent what the guest will receive. Photos must be current enough to be representative and the property must have the right to use them. Stock, materially altered, misleading or unrelated property photographs are not acceptable.
7. Premium supply standard. RoamWise is curated rather than an automatic open-listing board. Approval may require public-location verification, current property evidence, a remote/live walkthrough, cleanliness practice, guest-review evidence, transparent mandatory charges and a responsive owner/manager. Presence on any third-party OTA by itself neither qualifies nor disqualifies a property.
8. Reviews and guest experience. The property will not create, buy or solicit fake reviews. Repeated material complaints about cleanliness, safety, misrepresentation, undisclosed charges, refusal to honour confirmed terms or poor communication may lead to ranking reduction, temporary pause or delisting after review.
9. Availability and booking confirmation. Until real-time inventory is technically and contractually enabled, a RoamWise request is not a confirmed booking until the property confirms availability. The final pre-payment price, room, cancellation policy and mandatory charges must match what the guest accepts.
10. Payments and settlement. Payment routing, vendor settlement and refunds will operate only through methods separately enabled for the property. Provider KYC, tax treatment and payout timing may require additional documentation. RoamWise will not ask the property to place gateway secrets, bank passwords or card data in a public form.
11. Content licence. The property grants RoamWise a non-exclusive, revocable licence during the relationship to display approved property text, photographs and factual information for listing, marketing and guest communication. Ownership remains with the original rights holder.
12. Compliance. Each party remains responsible for laws, registrations, licences, taxes and consumer obligations applicable to its own role. The property is responsible for lawful operation of the accommodation and accuracy of invoices/charges it controls.
13. Suspension and termination. Either party may request to end the relationship. RoamWise may immediately pause new bookings where there is credible evidence of safety risk, fraud, material misrepresentation, repeated guest harm or unresolved pricing/content integrity issues. Existing guest, refund, dispute and settlement records are preserved.
14. Confidential information and data. Non-public commercial terms and personal data should be used only for the partnership and handled under applicable law and RoamWise's privacy policy. Public invitation links must not be used to collect sensitive identity or banking credentials.
15. Pilot nature; full agreement. This MOU records the intended pilot commercial framework. Any property-specific schedule, verified paid-plan activation, payment-provider agreement or later signed contract may add or supersede terms. Where enforceability, tax or local licensing is material, the parties should execute the final form appropriate to that property.
16. Governing framework. The relationship is intended to be governed by applicable laws of India. Any mandatory local jurisdiction or statutory consumer right continues to apply.
