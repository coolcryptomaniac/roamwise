# RoamWise Premium Supply Standard

Version: RW-PREMIUM-SUPPLY-2026-09-23-V1

RoamWise Stays is a curated marketplace. A property is not approved merely because it exists on another OTA or because an owner can create an account.

## Admission gates

Every live RoamWise property should pass all of these gates before direct bookings are enabled:

1. **Authority** — the applicant is the owner or an authorised manager.
2. **Real property/location** — the property exists at the stated location and the applicant can demonstrate access/control.
3. **Current representative photography** — listing photos materially match the rooms and common areas guests will receive; photo rights are confirmed.
4. **Pricing integrity** — the proposed RoamWise rate is checked against at least two like-for-like current public comparators. Differences are normalized for date, room type, occupancy, meals, cancellation terms, mandatory fees and taxes before conclusions are drawn.
5. **Guest-value pass-through** — the property does not inflate the RoamWise rate simply to absorb RoamWise's lower commission. When a verified comparator channel has a higher supplier commission, RoamWise expects a measurable guest benefit from the lower distribution cost.
6. **Transparent charges** — mandatory resort/property/cleaning/management charges are disclosed before payment.
7. **Cleanliness and service readiness** — a documented turnover/cleanliness practice and reachable owner/manager are required.
8. **Review/public evidence** — public review history is checked for recurring material complaints, without treating any single third-party platform as authoritative.
9. **Experience fit** — RoamWise prioritises distinctive, well-run stays with strong local character, food, nature, wellness, culture, design or service.
10. **Human approval** — no external feed, import or self-service submission automatically becomes a RoamWise Pick.

## Marketplace positioning

- RoamWise Picks are verified direct supply and appear before external fallback inventory.
- External OTA inventory may widen traveller choice, but it must not inherit the RoamWise Verified/Pick badge.
- Presence on Booking.com, Airbnb, MakeMyTrip, Agoda, OYO, Expedia or any other marketplace is evidence only; it is neither sufficient for approval nor an automatic reason to reject a property.
- RoamWise should not auto-import photos, ratings, ownership claims or room prices from an open marketplace and present them as verified facts.
- Prefer official property sources, direct owner evidence, current walkthroughs and multiple independent public references.

## Price audit protocol

For each new property, record at least two comparators:
- source and public URL;
- check timestamp;
- same stay dates and occupancy;
- same/sufficiently equivalent room class;
- meal inclusion;
- cancellation/refund terms;
- mandatory fees and tax treatment;
- public guest total or comparable nightly equivalent;
- supplier commission percentage only when the property provides reliable evidence of its actual contract.

A cheaper public rate is a **review flag**, not an automatic breach. An unexplained material premium, hidden mandatory fee or deliberate RoamWise markup remains pending until corrected.

If an actual comparator supplier commission is documented and is higher than the applicable RoamWise commission, the same-net audit reference is:

`comparator guest rate × (1 − comparator commission) ÷ (1 − RoamWise commission)`

This can estimate the RoamWise rate that preserves approximately the same supplier net while passing the lower distribution cost to the guest. It is an audit tool, not an MFN/exclusivity restriction.

## Ongoing trust

Pause/re-review a property when:
- photos or room facts are materially inaccurate;
- mandatory fees appear after confirmation;
- repeated guests report cleanliness/safety/misrepresentation;
- the property repeatedly rejects confirmed terms;
- public evidence materially conflicts with the listing;
- a material rate change appears designed to evade the guest-value commitment.

Existing bookings, refunds, disputes and settlement history must be preserved during any pause.
