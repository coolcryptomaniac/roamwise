Verify a Journey Passport

Paste a passport ID to confirm whether it is a genuine RoamWise travel record.

Why this can be trusted.
A Journey Passport is not a picture or a PDF — those can be edited by anyone. It is a record written into the RoamWise network by a signed-in member at the time of stamping. This page checks the ID directly against that network. If a record was not issued by RoamWise, it simply does not exist here, and no amount of copying the app can change that.
