Quick answers

How do I check if a road is open in Uttarakhand?

Check the Uttarakhand traffic police site and district disaster control rooms the morning you travel, and see RoamWise's road and safety status page for current closures, crowds and events with sources.

What is the Uttarakhand disaster helpline number?

Dial 1070 for the Uttarakhand State Disaster Management Authority helpline, or 112 for police, fire and ambulance.

When do the Char Dham temples close for winter in 2026?

Badrinath closes on 16 November 2026. Kedarnath and Yamunotri are expected to close on Bhai Dooj (11 November) and Gangotri on 10 November; confirm with the temple committees.
