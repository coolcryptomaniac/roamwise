Startup program memberships

Member of NVIDIA Inception

RoamWise Technologies is a member of NVIDIA Inception, a program for technology startups providing access to technical learning, ecosystem resources and eligible benefits. RoamWise added its AI travel platform to the program profile in November 2026.

We are evaluating NVIDIA technologies for future conversational AI inference, multilingual speech and retrieval workflows. NVIDIA Inception membership does not mean NVIDIA has invested in, endorsed or commercially partnered with RoamWise, nor does it imply that NVIDIA software or GPUs are deployed in the live product.

Read about the official NVIDIA Inception program. RoamWise uses its own brand identity on this page; NVIDIA brand assets are used only in accordance with NVIDIA Inception's specific member-branding permissions.

Google for Startups Cloud Program

RoamWise Technologies has been accepted into the Google for Startups Cloud Program (Start tier), which provides Google Cloud and Firebase credits and access to technical resources. Program membership does not mean Google has invested in, endorsed or partnered with RoamWise. About the program.

Sarvam Startup Program

RoamWise Technologies has been accepted into the Sarvam Startup Program, which gives access to Sarvam's Indian-language speech, translation and language APIs with startup credits. We use these for Hindi and Indian-language voice and translation in the travel copilot. Program membership does not mean Sarvam has invested in or endorsed RoamWise. About Sarvam.

Formal support and investment claims

RoamWise only uses wording such as “backed by,” “supported by,” “portfolio company,” “accelerated by,” or “partnered with” when there is a signed agreement, formal acceptance, investment, grant, awarded credits, or another verifiable basis for that wording.

Until then, companies whose products RoamWise uses are described as technology providers or ecosystem tools rather than investors or sponsors. This keeps our investor, customer and property-partner communications accurate.
