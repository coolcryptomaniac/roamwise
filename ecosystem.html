Startup programs we are evaluating

These are programs RoamWise can apply to or evaluate for credits, mentorship and technical support. They are not listed as current supporters unless RoamWise receives a documented acceptance, grant, credit award or program benefit.

  • Google for Startups Cloud Program — cloud/Firebase credits, technical and business support subject to eligibility and approval. Program page.
  • OpenAI for Startups — startup community and, for eligible partner-network companies, possible API credits and technical resources. Program page.
  • Claude Startups by Anthropic — startup program offering eligible founders Claude/API benefits and community resources. Program page.
  • Cloudflare for Startups — eligible startups can apply for infrastructure credits and technical support. Program page.
  • NVIDIA Inception — free startup program with technical, ecosystem and go-to-market benefits for eligible companies. Program page.
  • AWS Activate and Microsoft for Startups — additional cloud/startup-credit programs RoamWise may evaluate as infrastructure needs change.

Formal support and investment claims

RoamWise only uses wording such as “backed by,” “supported by,” “portfolio company,” “accelerated by,” or “partnered with” when there is a signed agreement, formal acceptance, investment, grant, awarded credits, or another verifiable basis for that wording.

Until then, companies whose products RoamWise uses are described as technology providers or ecosystem tools rather than investors or sponsors. This keeps our investor, customer and property-partner communications accurate.
