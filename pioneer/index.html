Rules that protect everyone

01 · ProofA real verified membership or settled transaction counts; repeat self-referrals, reversals and chargebacks do not.
02 · ValueBenefits are designed around real utility: itinerary history, discovery, trusted data, and helpful community reputation — never speculative tokens.
03 · BudgetAny future optional store credit or discount must be pre-approved, tied to realized margin and capped by an available promotional pool; otherwise it is zero.
04 · ContinuityPublished source, backups, signed releases and reviewable policy changes let a small team keep the essentials running. A website and regulated payments cannot be promised to run forever unattended.

The Satoshi-inspired part

Instead of an unaccountable founder deciding every reward, use a small, understandable policy: evidence before points, explicit spending caps, one member one unique verified identity, no transferable rewards, independent review of policy changes, and public rule versions. Members can help improve RoamWise without needing permission to own its operations.

No crypto asset, referral investment returns, shares, guaranteed discounts, transferable stored value or passive income is being offered. Published prices and legally required consumer refund rights remain separate from optional benefits.
