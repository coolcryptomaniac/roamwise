ABOUT JAGRITI

Delhi NCR artist, spiritual and contemporary.

Jagriti Mishra paints as Jagriti Creates. Her acrylic and texture work, canvases and murals draw on Indian deities, spiritual and mythological subjects, including Maa Saraswati, Maa Kali, Lord Shiva and Shakti, and on traditional cultural aesthetics. She also makes digital illustrations.

She shares her process and painting tutorials on YouTube and posts regularly on Instagram (about 49K followers) and Threads (about 8K).
