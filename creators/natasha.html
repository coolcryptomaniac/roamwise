Her story

She did not wait to be taught. She began.

Natasha Guleria loved painting as a child and never stopped. Nobody handed her a method: she taught herself, canvas by canvas, until the work could stand in a room and hold it.

She began selling her art after a friend’s challenge, and what started as a dare became Bhavitavya Kala, a studio of spiritual originals, archival prints, wood-carving prints and personal commissions. Her subjects are Shiva in meditation above snow peaks, Kailash, the Sri Yantra in blue and gold, the Tridev, kundalini, and portraits that catch a face mid-laugh.

Away from the easel she sings, vlogs her travels and treks, and models. She was crowned Miss Himachal in 2019. Through all of it she shares her process openly, which is why about 49,000 people follow her on Instagram to watch a painting arrive.
