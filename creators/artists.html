Before you pay

Buyers must independently verify the artist, artwork ownership and authenticity, original/print/digital format, condition, total price, payment recipient, delivery and refund terms before any transaction. Ask for recent artwork evidence and keep written records. You transact directly with the artist; RoamWise does not hold these payments or guarantee authenticity, delivery or refunds.

ABOUT NATASHA

Artist, creator and Miss Himachal 2019.

Natasha Guleria is the self-taught artist behind Bhavitavya Kala, painting spiritual works, customised paintings and portraits from Himachal Pradesh. She loved painting as a child, began selling her art after a friend’s challenge, and has built an audience of about 49K on Instagram by sharing her process.

Away from the easel she sings, vlogs her travels and treks on YouTube, and models.

Laurels & milestones

  • Miss Himachal 2019. Crowned at the state contest in Hamirpur, reported by The Tribune on 30 December 2019 and announced by the MV Miss Himachal pageant.
  • Originals that found homes. Several of her original paintings, including Sri Yantra — Shakti and Shiv Parivar, have sold from the studio.
  • A growing audience. About 49K Instagram followers (October 2026) for her paintings and process videos.
