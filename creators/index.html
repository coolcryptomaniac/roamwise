PROTECTED BY DESIGN

Funded is a state,
not a marketing word.

RoamWise does not hold campaign money in its normal operating bank account. The production flow uses an approved payment aggregator marketplace product or a bank-led escrow arrangement, with creator KYC, webhook verification, immutable events, payout holds and dispute controls.
