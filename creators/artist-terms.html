ARTIST TERMS

Terms for artists on RoamWise

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Governed by the laws of India · Read with the Terms of use and Refund policy.

1. What RoamWise does

RoamWise shows your page and paintings to travellers and collectors and helps them contact you. We are not a party to any sale. We do not take payment for your paintings, hold buyer money, ship work or issue invoices for your sales.

2. Your work and your rights

  • You own your work, or have the right to show it. You keep full ownership and copyright.
  • You give RoamWise a non-exclusive permission to display your name, photos, paintings, prices and links on RoamWise pages and in promotion of your page. You can withdraw it by asking us to remove your page.
  • No exclusivity. You may sell anywhere else, including on your own site and Instagram.

3. Honest listings

  • Show only work you made. Mark prints and reproductions as prints. Do not present AI-generated images as hand-painted work.
  • Do not copy another artist, and do not upload photos of other people without their permission.
  • Keep price and status (Available, Reserved, Sold) up to date. Prices on your page are what you will honour for a reasonable time.
  • Say clearly what the buyer gets: medium, size, framing, and what is included.

4. Selling directly

You and the buyer agree price, payment, packing, delivery, returns and any tax or invoice. You are responsible for these, including GST where it applies to you. Good practice we ask artists to follow: confirm price and delivery in writing before payment, pack safely, share tracking, and agree any return terms in advance. Many artists treat made-to-order and commissioned work as non-returnable. Say so before the buyer pays.

5. Fees, thank-you and the future

  • Listing is free. The first 100 artists are founding artists with every feature at no cost.
  • From artist 101 we plan paid tiers: most artists run on autopilot (self-serve), and full hands-on support is a Premium tier. Prices are one-time, never auto-renewed, and shown before you pay. Founding artists keep what they were promised when they joined, and we will tell you in writing before any change to it.
  • There is no fixed commission or fee on your sales today.
  • If a buyer came to you through RoamWise, you may choose to share a thank-you contribution. It is optional, has no fixed amount or percentage, and never affects your listing. We may change how we approach this over time.
  • In future RoamWise may introduce a small commission on sales that come through RoamWise. If we do, we will tell you in writing before it applies, you can choose to leave, and it will not apply to sales made earlier.

6. Refund of a contribution

A thank-you contribution is voluntary. If you sent one by mistake, write to support@roamwise.co.in within 7 days and we will return it.

7. Removal and complaints

  • You can ask us to edit or remove your page any time. We aim to do it within 2 working days.
  • We may remove a listing that breaks these terms, infringes someone's rights, is misleading, or draws repeated genuine complaints from buyers.
  • To report a copied image, a wrong price or any other problem, email support@roamwise.co.in with the page link.

8. Your details

Only what you share is shown: name, studio, location, links, WhatsApp (if you choose), bio and paintings. See the Privacy policy.
