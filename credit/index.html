Questions

Is this an investment or a wallet top-up?

Neither. You are paying for a one-year RoamWise membership: Pro, your founding member number, the Founders Wall, priority help and the other perks listed for your tier. No interest, returns, shares or profit share. RoamWise does not hold your money as a balance.

What are RoamWise Coins?

A free loyalty reward that comes with your membership. Each Coin takes ₹1 off a RoamWise stay, for up to 10% of that booking. Coins have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, sold or transferred, and end one year after you get them.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of joining and before you use any Coins: reply to your welcome email and we refund the full fee to your original payment; the Coins and perks are then removed. After that the membership is not refundable, because you have had the service. Any booking you make is refundable under that booking's own policy, back to the way you paid.

Why is GST included?

A membership is a service RoamWise provides, so it carries GST. The price you see is the full price; your invoice shows the GST.

How do I get the Pro and other perks?

We set them up for you within 48 hours of payment and email you. Your founding member number is in your welcome email.

I bought a credit pack before. What happens to it?

Nothing changes: it works until its expiry date and anything unused is refunded to your original payment, exactly as promised.
