Questions

Is this an investment?

No. It is prepaid credit for RoamWise trips: no interest, no returns, no shares and no profit share. Your benefit is the bonus credit and the member perks. If RoamWise ever raises money, that will be a separate, properly documented offer.

What happens if I don't use it?

We email you 30 and 7 days before it ends. On day 360, whatever is left of the money you paid goes back to your original payment method automatically. We never keep it, and we never roll it over without asking you. Unused bonus credit ends that day.

Can I get my money back early?

Yes. Open your wallet and choose "send to bank" on your pack credit. The unused part of what you paid is refunded to your original payment method, and the matching share of the bonus is given up.

What can I spend it on?

Every stay, package and wedding you can book on roamwise.co.in, for you or anyone you book for. It cannot be exchanged for cash.

How do I get the Pro and other perks?

We set them up for you within 48 hours of payment and email you. Your founding member number is in your welcome email.

Why at most 10% bonus?

So every trip you take with credit still pays the hotels and guides fairly. We would rather give you a sustainable bonus every year than a big one once.
