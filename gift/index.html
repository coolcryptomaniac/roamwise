Questions

Where can I spend it?

On everything RoamWise sells and invoices itself: stays and packages, the RoamWise shop and memberships. A few premium stays are booked on the property's behalf (checkout says so); those are paid by card or UPI, and your Coins still give up to 10% off.

Can I get my money back?

Yes, any unused amount goes back to the payment it came from: automatically when it ends after 360 days, or earlier if you write to support@roamwise.co.in. It is never paid out as cash or to someone else.

Is there GST on adding money?

No. Adding money or buying a voucher is not a purchase yet. GST is on the invoice for what you buy with it.

Is there a limit?

A Trip Wallet holds up to ₹50,000. Vouchers are ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹5,000 or ₹10,000 each.
