Common scams in Uttarakhand

"Confirmed" helicopter seats

Ads, Facebook pages and WhatsApp numbers offering guaranteed Kedarnath seats. Real tickets are only on IRCTC HeliYatra, and demand is 3–4 times the seats.

Paid "registration"

Someone offers to register you for Char Dham for a fee. Registration is free on the official portal and at counters.

Fake GMVN / KMVN rest houses

Look-alike sites using the state tourism names. Book state rest houses only on gmvnonline.com or kmvn.gov.in.

Too-good homestay deals

Copied photos and a full advance to a personal UPI ID. Call the number on Google Maps, read recent reviews, and pay only a small advance.

Quick answers

How do I know if a Kedarnath helicopter booking site is fake?

Genuine Kedarnath helicopter tickets are sold only on IRCTC HeliYatra (heliyatra.irctc.co.in). Any other website, Facebook page, WhatsApp number or agent selling them is fake.

Is Char Dham registration free?

Yes. Registration on the official Uttarakhand Tourism portal and at official counters is free. Nobody needs a payment to register you.

Where do I report an online booking fraud?

Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in as soon as possible, and inform your bank.
