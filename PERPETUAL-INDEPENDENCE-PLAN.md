# RoamWise perpetual independence plan

This is the product and operating contract for building a crores-scale
RoamWise without turning the founder into a consultant, call-centre manager,
or hostage to one customer, investor, model vendor or destination.

The goal is not a guaranteed forecast. The goal is a system whose revenue can
compound for ten years while fixed work, supplier concentration and variable
compute cost stay bounded.

## Non-negotiable promises

- Existing purchases and entitlements remain valid.
- The ₹100 Founder lifetime offer remains lifetime access and keeps the full
  product toolkit promised at purchase.
- The 500 NMIMS lifetime activations remain reserved within the 1,000-seat
  Founder cap.
- Lifetime never means an uncapped third-party API bill. Smart Planner and
  bring-your-own AI are unlimited; RoamWise-funded paid-model calls use a
  monthly fair-use allowance.
- No lifetime promise is made for partner desks, widgets, white-label hosting,
  live support, bespoke writing or other cloud-heavy B2B services.
- Commission is charged only on completed bookings and commercial terms are
  snapshotted rather than changed after confirmation.

The former ₹999 founder consultation is deliberately removed from the public
store. A self-serve Trip Review uses the product instead of selling founder
hours.

## Ten-year control loop

The Admin → Business page is the operating cockpit. It starts from measured
MRR and owner-entered costs, then keeps all growth, churn, other revenue,
variable-cost and target figures visibly labelled as assumptions.

Review monthly:

1. MRR, churn and contribution margin.
2. Break-even monthly revenue and cash collected from one-time products.
3. Managed-AI usage and cost per paid account.
4. Country, property and calendar-month booking concentration.
5. Revenue share from the largest customer and largest channel.
6. Founder hours spent on support, sales and fulfilment.

Operating guardrails:

- Pause paid acquisition when payback exceeds 12 months.
- Do not enable a managed-AI provider without authentication, a fixed
  founder-selected model, monthly metering and hard prompt/output caps.
- Keep any one customer below 10% of revenue and any one property below 15%
  of direct-booking commission.
- Add supply across at least three independent regions before treating
  direct-booking revenue as resilient.
- Hire or automate only after a repeated queue is measured; do not create a
  permanent role for an unproven workflow.
- Keep payments, identity, tax and emergency response with regulated or
  qualified providers; RoamWise orchestrates but does not pretend to insure,
  rescue or hold money without the required approval.

## Crore milestone arithmetic

₹1 crore annual revenue is roughly ₹8.33 lakh monthly revenue. At ₹2,499/year,
that would require about 4,002 annual Pro subscriptions if subscriptions were
the only engine. A mixed portfolio needs fewer consumer subscriptions because
partner subscriptions, completed-booking commission, prepaid widgets and
white-label plans contribute independently.

The admin model should be used to test the actual mix. A target is considered
operationally healthy only when it is reached after variable API/gateway costs
and owner-entered fixed costs—not merely as gross booking value or one-time
cash collected.
