**Date: 22 Nov 2026.** Neither company has agreed to a partnership.
Do not display CoinDCX logo or imply sponsorship/authorisation.

## Sell a service first

For a nominal **1,000 employees**, quote an annual per-allocated-seat
travel software licence at **₹150–₹300/seat/year** (₹1.5L–₹3L total);
include fixed AI fair-use and seat/domain verification, with a usage/servicing
cost budget. Do not promise unlimited paid AI in return for an uncapped licence.

Then sell separate, supplier-confirmed department SOWs: typical group
30–80 staff, leadership 15–25, optional workation and employee perk offers.
The exact prices and margins require supplier quotations.

Illustrative model in `corporate/enterprise-core.mjs`:
- 1,000 allocated seats × ₹250 = **₹2.5L proposed annual licence**, not profit.
- 4 department trips × 60 staff × ₹12k = **₹28.8L booking value**.
- 2 leadership trips × 20 staff × ₹25k = **₹10L booking value**.
- 24 workation bookings × ₹20k = **₹4.8L booking value**.
- 50 personal bookings × ₹15k = **₹7.5L booking value**.
- **₹51.1L illustrative annual travel GMV**, plus ₹2.5L potential
  software billing = ₹53.6L gross *client expenditures*; NOT revenue or cash held.
- Illustrative 10% contribution target on travel bookings: **₹5.11L**
  IF every trip materializes AND all quote-level direct expenses still allow 10%.
  Do not add this to software gross billings as "net profit".

## Commercial routes, in priority order

### A — CoinDCX the employer: HR/procurement pilot
1. Seek a 30–60-person departmental 2N/3D offsite request, and offer an
   annual employee travel licence alongside it.
2. Obtain written permission for a tenant-specific invitation and
   work-domain verification. Do not create a generic shareable
   `COMPANY1000` claim code; it invites abuse.
3. Demo an admin-controlled ledger of `seat_allocated`, `seat_claimed`,
   `seat_revoked`, fair-use AI quota and renewal. Existing consumer
   NMIMS coupons are **not** proof of an authorised enterprise programme.
4. Issue a written quote with rate, group size, cancellation, tax, privacy,
   service-level expectations, safety, verified venue and per-stay margin.
5. Payment only after procurement/finance approval. A refundable advance
   remains a liability, **not unrestricted working capital**.

### B — CoinDCX community: sponsored travel *perks* (potential)
CoinDCX publicly describes community perks and rewards; this does NOT
constitute a partner API or agreement. Offer a marketing collaboration:
customer receives a single-use offer for a quoted stay or experience,
conditioned on actual eligibility, with RoamWise paid by the sponsor
or from a priced stay where supplier economics pass.
Offer does not require customers to trade, buy, or transfer crypto.
Do not claim discounted room rates unless supplier approved.

### C — Optional digital-asset payment or rewards rail (do not build yet)
Explore **only** if CoinDCX's compliance, payments, security, legal and
procurement teams approve a concrete product, supported API and entity
responsibilities. The available public CoinDCX APIs primarily support trading;
they do not by themselves authorise third-party travel-token credits or
crypto remittance to hotel suppliers.

Boundary: RoamWise quotes, accepts approved fiat payments and pays travel
suppliers in INR through ordinary invoicing. A partner would own any
customer-side crypto KYC, tax obligations, wallet control, custody, transfer
and conversion, if lawful and contractually supported. No RoamWise VDA keys,
coins, on-chain redemption, yield, staking, returns or native token.

## Credit profit checks before any offer

An INR **₹5L advance + ₹50k conditional service-value bonus** consumes
₹5.5L face value if fully redeemed. On ₹5L *earned* cash, 10% minimum
contribution requires **maximum ₹4.5L ALL-IN variable costs**.
If variable operations cost ₹10k, suppliers must cost at most ₹4.4L
or 80% of face value. Seven-percent room commission cannot reliably fund
a 10% bonus and retain 10% on the same service.

Deposits are NOT revenue when refundable or when services are unearned.
Use approved milestone invoicing and supplier holdbacks instead of
selling an unrestricted multi-merchant stored-value wallet. Even a
"closed-loop" claim needs an expert evaluation of the actual merchant flow.

## Implementation guardrails

- Enterprise scenario stays client-side; its "use in enquiry" button
  inserts an editable summary into the existing lead notes. No corporate
  service is provisioned, and the form doesn't auto-bill or activate codes.
- Web3 section explicitly says there is no CoinDCX agreement and no token.
- This is a sales prototype awaiting business verification and all
  approvals; it must not be marketed as live corporate enrolment.
- Keep CoinDCX-specific contractual details out of the public static site.
- Before implementation beyond enquiry, require separate signoffs for:
  (1) procurement/SOW, (2) treasury and refund accounting, (3) partner
  due diligence, (4) regulatory and tax classification, (5) security and
  data-protection review, (6) supplier confirmation.
