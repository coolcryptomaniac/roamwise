📍 Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1Arrive Manali. Check into hostel (₹400–500). Evening at Mall Road. Dal makhani dinner at a dhaba.
Day 2Rohtang Pass permit (₹500 online). Shared cab up and back (₹300). Snow activities. Return by 4pm.
Day 3Old Manali walk, Hadimba Temple, solang valley. Packed lunch saves ₹200 vs eating up there.
Day 4Naggar Castle detour on the way out or Vashisht hot springs before departure.

❓ Common Questions

Is ₹15,000 enough for 4 days in Manali?
Yes, comfortably. Budget travellers spend ₹7,000–9,500 including the Delhi bus. ₹15,000 gives room for a private room, a better restaurant meal each day, and a snowmobile ride at Solang.
What is the cheapest way to reach Manali?
Overnight Volvo bus from Delhi ISBT Kashmere Gate — booked on redBus 2–3 weeks ahead, seats cost ₹900–1,400 one way. Avoid booking on arrival, rates spike 3x in peak season.
Which months are cheapest in Manali?
June is peak season but shorter due to school holidays — September and early October are quieter, 30–40% cheaper on rooms, and roads are clear after monsoon.
