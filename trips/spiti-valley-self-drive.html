Self-Drive Spiti Valley from Chandigarh — Road Route & Budget

The complete self-drive Spiti Valley circuit from Chandigarh via Shimla–Kinnaur and return via Manali — road conditions, petrol stops, and total cost.
📅 9 days  ·  💰 Budget ₹35,000  ·  ⏱ Best time: June–September

📍 Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1Chandigarh → Shimla (120km, NH5). Stay Shimla.
Day 2Shimla → Sangla Valley via Kinnaur (230km, 8hr). Steep but paved until Tapri.
Day 3Sangla → Chitkul (28km) — India's last village, 3,450m. Return Sangla. Fill up at Tapri.
Day 4Sangla → Kaza via Nako and Tabo (150km, 6–8hr). Unpaved from Pooh onward.
Days 5–6Kaza base. Key Monastery, Kibber, Pin Valley National Park. Fill tank in Kaza.
Day 7Kaza → Losar → Kunzum Pass (4,590m) → Chandrataal Lake. Camp at Chandrataal.
Day 8Chandrataal → Manali via Rohtang (110km, 6–7hr). Rohtang permit required online.
Day 9Manali → Chandigarh (310km, 7hr).

❓ Common Questions

What car do you need for Spiti Valley self-drive?
A petrol SUV with good ground clearance — Mahindra Thar, Scorpio, XUV700, or Fortuner. Do NOT attempt in a hatchback or sedan. The Kunzum Pass section has boulders, river crossings, and zero guardrails.
Where do you fill petrol in Spiti Valley?
Fill completely in Shimla, Rampur, Tapri, and Kaza. There are NO petrol pumps between Pooh and Kaza (200km). Carry a 10L jerry can from Tapri minimum.
Is a 4WD/4x4 mandatory for Spiti?
Not mandatory for the Shimla route in June–September if dry. However, 4x4 is essential after rain, and mandatory for the Manali route via Kunzum which has multiple stream crossings.
