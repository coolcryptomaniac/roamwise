Solo Backpacker 7 Days in Goa — Real Itinerary & Budget

A 7-day solo backpacker itinerary for Goa — covering North vs South Goa, when to go for fewer crowds, and how to do it under ₹12,000.
📅 7 days  ·  💰 Budget ₹12,000  ·  ⏱ Best time: November–February (avoid Christmas–New Year week for crowds and pricing)

📍 Day-by-Day Itinerary

Days 1–2Arrive Goa. Base in Anjuna or Arambol (North Goa). Explore Vagator and Chapora Fort. Cheap shacks at Anjuna beach.
Days 3–4Rent a scooter (₹300/day). Cover Baga, Calangute, Fort Aguada. Flea market if Wednesday or Saturday.
Day 5Bus to South Goa (₹40). Colva, Palolem, Butterfly Beach — all quieter, cheaper, and cleaner.
Days 6–7Old Goa churches (UNESCO, free entry), Panjim Latin Quarter, Goa State Museum. Overnight train out.

❓ Common Questions

How much does 7 days in Goa cost for a solo traveller?
₹10,000–14,000 is realistic covering transport from most Indian cities, a clean hostel bed or cheap room, food, and scooter rental. December–January rates are 50–80% higher.
Is Goa safe for solo travellers?
Yes, one of the safest states in India for solo travel — and for women travellers specifically. Anjuna and Palolem have the most established backpacker infrastructure.
When is Goa least crowded?
October–November (post-monsoon, pre-Christmas) and February–early March are the sweet spots — beaches are clean, weather is good, and rooms cost 30–50% less than the Christmas–New Year peak.
