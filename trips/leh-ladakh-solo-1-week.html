📍 Day-by-Day Itinerary

Days 1–2Arrive Leh by flight (altitude 3,500m). Do NOT exert. Rest, hydrate, short walk only. Most AMS hits on day 2.
Day 3Acclimatised: Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa, local bazaar. Arrange Inner Line Permits (Pangong, Nubra).
Day 4Shared jeep to Pangong Tso via Chang La (5,360m). Overnight at lakeside camps.
Day 5Pangong sunrise. Return via different route or continue to Nubra Valley via Shyok.
Days 6–7Nubra Valley — Diskit Monastery, Hunder sand dunes, Bactrian camel ride. Return Leh. Departure flight.

❓ Common Questions

Can you do Leh Ladakh solo without a car?
Yes — shared jeep tours from Leh's main market for Pangong and Nubra are available daily in season (June–Sep). A solo traveller joins a group of 4–6, splitting fuel costs. Total for both circuits is ₹3,500–5,000.
Is Inner Line Permit required for Leh Ladakh?
Yes, for Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley, and Dah Hanu. Apply online at lahdc.nic.in or at the SDM office in Leh. Costs ₹200–400 per area, ready in 30 minutes.
How bad is altitude sickness in Ladakh?
About 40% of visitors get moderate AMS symptoms on arrival. The standard protocol: 2 full rest days in Leh before any high-altitude excursion. Diamox (acetazolamide) helps — consult a doctor before the trip.
