A travel planning tool. It provides itineraries, cost estimates, crowd patterns, mapped places, weather and general travel guidance, drawn from open data sources including Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA), OpenStreetMap contributors and Open-Meteo.

Cost figures are modelled estimates, not quotations.
