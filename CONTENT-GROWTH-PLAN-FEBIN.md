# RoamWise — 3-Month Content & Growth Task Plan (Febin)

> Owner: Febin ("The Warrior") · Product: [roamwise.co.in](https://www.roamwise.co.in) — free, India-first AI travel planner · Channel focus: Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp
> Cadence: weekly tasks, grouped by month. Check off as you go. Report metrics every Friday.

## Ground rules (read once, apply always)

- **Brand voice = honest, anti-hype, indie.** Match the site: "Plan trips that survive contact with reality." No fake urgency, no "top 10 secret spots" clickbait, no inflated claims. We win on *usefulness*, not hype.
- **Always lead with a real feature or a real number.** Crowd calendars (least-crowded month), honest INR budgets, scam warnings, group cost-splitting, low-carbon ledger, offline access, one-time ₹100 Pro unlock (never call it a subscription).
- **Ailon Tusk is not an LLM** — it's data + rules. Never market RoamWise as "ChatGPT for travel." Say "data-driven trip planner."
- **Every post ends with one clear CTA:** "Plan it free at roamwise.co.in."
- **Track everything.** Views, saves, shares, profile clicks, link clicks, and app opens (use a UTM link per channel).
- **Repurpose ruthlessly.** 1 YouTube video → 3 Reels → 5 LinkedIn posts → 3 WhatsApp broadcasts.

## MONTH 1 — Foundation & Awareness

**Goal:** Set up all channels properly, lock the visual/voice template, and ship a consistent baseline of content. Targets: 12 Reels, 12 LinkedIn posts, 4 YouTube videos, WhatsApp broadcast list of 100+.

Build a reusable Reel template (caption font Outfit, gold accent `#E8BA6C`, safe zones). Ship 2 Reels: (1) "Type one line, get a full trip" screen-recording demo; (2) "The least-crowded month for [popular place]" using the crowd calendar.

## Content pillars (rotate across all channels)
1. **Honest budgets** — real INR breakdowns, hidden costs.
2. **Crowd calendars** — least-crowded month, avoid peak prices.
3. **Scam warnings** — specific tricks + how the app flags them.

5. **Low-carbon** — emissions avoided, credible data sources.
6. **Build-in-public** — solo founder story, transparency, "what we won't build."

## Definition of done (every task)
