All journeys

Char Dham Yatra

Four Himalayan shrines, one carefully prepared journey. This page covers Uttarakhand’s Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath circuit.

Panch Kedar

Five Shiva shrines across the Garhwal Himalaya: Kedarnath, Madhmaheshwar, Tungnath, Rudranath and Kalpeshwar. Prepare for each trail as its own journey.

Kumbh · four sacred river cities

Explore Prayagraj, Haridwar, Nashik–Trimbakeshwar and Ujjain. Kumbh is a rotating event, not a permanent festival at every city.

Mata Vaishno Devi

Plan your Katra journey with the Shrine Board’s registration and service notices close at hand.

Kashi Vishwanath

A Varanasi visit centred on Shiva, the ghats and a considerate plan for busy pedestrian spaces.

Tirumala · Tirupati

Prepare for a Tirumala visit using TTD’s official darshan, accommodation and news links.

Ayodhya · Ram Janmabhoomi

A Sarayu-side city of Shri Ram devotion. Plan your Ram Mandir darshan around the Trust’s own notices and a careful walking plan.

Dwarka · Somnath

Two sacred coastal shrines of Gujarat: Dwarkadhish, the Krishna temple at Dwarka, and Somnath, the first of the twelve Jyotirlingas.

Puri · Konark

Odisha’s Jagannath Dham at Puri, with the Sun Temple at Konark nearby. Prepare for each place as its own visit.

Amarnath Yatra

A high-altitude Himalayan cave shrine of Shiva in Jammu and Kashmir. The yatra is a regulated seasonal journey with its own rules.

Shirdi Sai Baba

Prepare a Shirdi visit to the Samadhi Mandir using the Sansthan’s own darshan, accommodation and notice links.

Bodh Gaya

The Mahabodhi Temple complex in Bihar, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a place of quiet for pilgrims of many traditions.

Rameswaram · Madurai

Two great temple towns of Tamil Nadu: Rameswaram, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, and Madurai, home of the Meenakshi temple.

Kasar Devi · Almora hills

A quiet circuit of hill shrines around Almora in Kumaon: Kasar Devi, Chitai Golu Devta and the ancient Katarmal Sun Temple. Best taken slowly, early in the day.

Jageshwar Dham

A cluster of ancient Shiva temples set in a deodar forest valley near Almora. A place for an unhurried morning rather than a checklist.

Adi Kailash · Om Parvat

A remote high-Himalayan journey in Pithoragarh district towards Adi (Chhota) Kailash and Om Parvat, close to the Tibet border. Quiet, demanding and permit-based.

Kailash Mansarovar Yatra

Mount Kailash and Lake Mansarovar in Tibet, sacred to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Bon followers. Indian pilgrims travel through the government-organised yatra, not by independent booking.
