# Content & growth — Febin's 3-month brief for RoamWise

This is the running task sheet for **Febin** (owner of Reels, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp). It covers **three months**, week by week. It is not a wishlist — it is a cadence you can actually hit solo, tied to what RoamWise genuinely does better than everyone else.

Read `SEO-BRAND-PLAN.md`, `CREATOR-OUTREACH.md` and `COMPETITOR-WATCH.md` alongside this — the content here should feed those, not fight them.

---

## The one thing to remember

RoamWise's edge is **honesty**. Real local prices, real scam warnings, real crowd calendars, and itineraries that work offline. That's it. Global planners (Mindtrip, Layla, Wanderlog, Google/Gemini, TripIt) invent confident nonsense; we don't.

So the rule for every single post, reel, video and broadcast is:

**Every piece of content must prove one honest, specific, useful thing a tourist would otherwise get wrong.** No vibes, no drone-shot montages with zero information. If a viewer can't screenshot it and use it on a real trip, don't post it.

## The weekly baseline (holds for all 3 months)

| Channel | Cadence | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | 4 / week | 15–30s vertical, big captions, one fact per reel |
| **LinkedIn** | 3 / week | 1 founder-voice story, 1 data/insight post, 1 build-in-public update |
| **YouTube** | 1 long / week + 2 Shorts | Long = a real itinerary or a "what it should cost" breakdown |
| **WhatsApp** | 2 broadcasts / week + daily community reply | 1 tip drop, 1 guide/blog link; keep the group warm |

That's ~9 posts + 1 video + 2 broadcasts a week. If a week is heavy, protect the **Reels and the YouTube long** first — they compound; the rest supports them.

## How we measure

Vanity metrics lie. Track these instead, logged every Friday:

| Channel | Primary metric | Month-1 target | Month-2 target | Month-3 target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | Saves + shares per reel | 20+ avg | 60+ avg | 150+ avg |
| LinkedIn | Profile → site clicks | 100 / mo | 300 / mo | 600 / mo |
| YouTube | Watch-through % + subs | 40% / +150 subs | 45% / +400 subs | 50% / +800 subs |
| WhatsApp | Link clicks + group size | 200 clicks / 150 members | 500 / 400 | 1,000 / 800 |
| **Whole funnel** | **App installs / Pro unlocks attributed to content** | Instrument it | 50 installs | 150 installs / 20 Pro |

Put a UTM on every link (`?utm_source=reel`, `utm_source=li`, etc.) and use one trackable link in the WhatsApp broadcasts. If we can't attribute it, we can't scale it.

## Content pillars (every post is one of these five)

1. **"What it should actually cost"** — auto fares, food, stays, tickets, by city/route. Our anti-overcharging spine.
2. **"Don't get scammed"** — commission drivers, DCC card trick, rental-damage scams, fake-guide hustles. One mechanic per post.
3. **"Go when it's empty"** — crowd calendars, least-crowded months, shoulder-season plays.
4. **"Plan it in one line"** — show Ailon Tusk turning *"chill 4 days near Rishikesh under 12k"* into a real day-by-day plan with a rupee budget.
5. **"Travel smart, India edition"** — IRCTC Tatkal 10am timing, hub-and-spoke routing, Volvo vs flight, EV road trips, offline mode, fair cost-splitting for group trips.

Rotate pillars so the feed never feels one-note. Pillar 1 and 2 are our sharpest hooks — lead with them.

**Theme: "The honest travel account." Establish what RoamWise stands for.** Nobody knows us yet, so every post is a first impression. Volume + consistency + a crystal-clear identity.

- Set up UTM links and a link-in-bio (guides + app install).

**Weekly focus:**
- **Week 1** — Pillar 1 (costs). Reels: "What an auto *should* cost in [Delhi / Jaipur / Manali / Rishikesh]." YouTube long: *"How much a 4-day Rishikesh trip really costs — line by line."*

- **Week 3** — Pillar 3 (crowds). Reels: "The least-crowded month to visit [place]." YouTube long: *"Best months to travel in 2026 — ranked by crowd, not by blog."*
- **Week 4** — Pillar 4 (the product). Reels: screen-record Ailon Tusk building a plan from one line. YouTube long: *"I asked RoamWise to plan a ₹12k Himalaya trip — here's the day-by-day."*

**LinkedIn (all month):** founder-story angle — solo founder, Almora, no team/office/investors, why an anti-hype travel tool. Post the "why we refuse dark patterns" manifesto. Build-in-public: subscriber/install numbers, honestly.

**WhatsApp:** launch the "RoamWise Travel Tips" broadcast. Twice a week: one original tip + one link to an existing guide/blog. Personally reply to everyone who joins.

## The repurposing engine (one idea, four channels)

Never make four things. Make one, cut it four ways:

1. **Idea** (e.g. "auto fare in Jaipur should be ₹___").
2. **Reel** — the 20s punchy version.
3. **YouTube Short** — same cut, or bundle 5 fares into one long video.
4. **LinkedIn** — the same fact as a "here's the number and why it matters" insight post.
5. **WhatsApp** — the fact as a one-line tip + the link to the full guide.

One shoot, a week of content. That's how a solo owner survives this cadence.

## Guardrails — stay on brand

RoamWise refuses dark patterns, and so does the content:

- **No fake urgency, no fake scarcity.** If the founder unlock has a real cap, state the real cap. Never invent a countdown.
- **No confident invention.** If we don't have the number, we don't post the number. Verify against the guides/blog data.
- **No "areas to avoid" fear-mongering.** We warn about *mechanics* (scams, overcharging), never smear places or people.
- **No forced-signup theatre.** Sell the honest product honestly.
- **Founder voice, first person, plain language.** Slightly irreverent is fine ("built in the Himalayas 🏔️"), hype is not.
- **India-first, Hindi + English** where it fits the audience.

If a post would embarrass us in a "RoamWise is the honest one" article, don't post it.

## The Friday ritual (every week, all 3 months)

1. Log the week's metrics in the tracker (saves, clicks, subs, group size, installs).
2. Note the single best and single worst performer, and one sentence on why.
3. Bank next week's ideas from the content bank + this week's comments/DMs.
4. Post one honest build-in-public line on LinkedIn.
5. Send the two WhatsApp broadcasts.

Consistency beats brilliance here. Hit the baseline every week, let the data pick the winners, and pour the next month into whatever's already working.
