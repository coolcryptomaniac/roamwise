RoamWise Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026 · RoamWise, Almora, India

RoamWise ("we", "our") is built by Mohit Pandey in Almora, India. This policy explains what we collect, how ads and cookies work, and the choices you have — including specific rights for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

What we collect

RoamWise runs primarily on your device. Searches, XP, saved lists, profile fields and journey logs are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. If you create an account, we store via Google Firebase: your email address, display name, optional profile details you choose to enter (such as a WhatsApp number or travel preferences), and your Pro membership status. We do not sell your personal data. Weather uses approximate IP-based location, fetched transiently to answer your request and never stored. RoamWise also records anonymous daily totals for product events such as visits, searches, and Kasar festival enquiry-link taps; these counters do not include an email, account ID, precise location, or the city entered into the optional festival route field. A city entered there is added to the Ailon Tusk request on your device and is not retained as an analytics value. Milan Heights WhatsApp enquiries are guest-initiated and go directly to the hotel with a prefilled RoamWise referral message. RoamWise counts anonymous WhatsApp-link taps, not message contents, guest identity, or completed bookings.

Stay requests and WhatsApp

When you ask RoamWise to pass a stay request to a property (from your trip list or a room enquiry), we save your name, mobile number, the dates and number of guests you gave, any note you wrote, and which properties you asked about. You must tell us first that we may contact you on WhatsApp or by phone about that request; we use your number only to reply to that request and to connect you with the property, not for promotions. The request is visible to RoamWise administrators and is shared with the property you asked about so it can reply. We keep it for 12 months and then delete it automatically. To have it deleted sooner or to withdraw your consent, email support@roamwise.co.in from the address on your account or quote your mobile number. If a request cannot be sent, the app tells you so and nothing is saved.

Booking emails and trip reminders

When you send a stay request, RoamWise can email you an acknowledgement at the address on your account, and, for a stay the property has confirmed, remind you 3 days and 1 day before check-in (by push notification first, and by email only if no device received it). These are service messages about your own booking, not marketing, and each is sent once. Emails are sent from RoamWise’s own mail account through a relay we control, and are used for nothing else. We record only that a message was sent, with its time, on the booking.

Shared learned answers

To make Ailon Tusk faster for everyone, generic travel questions (those that do not mention you, your contacts or numbers) and Tusk’s plain-text answers can be saved in RoamWise’s cloud storage under a short code made from the question. Only that code and the plain-text answer are stored; your question text, name, account, IP address and device are not. Answers that contain links, emails, phone numbers or your remembered home city are never shared. Entries expire after 45 days, and a thumbs-down on a shared answer deletes it for everyone. You can switch this off at any time by telling Tusk “stop sharing learnings”; Tusk then neither shares nor uses other travellers’ saved answers. Reply animations are a display setting on your device (say “fast mode” for plain instant text).

Ailon Tusk stay requests

When you ask Ailon Tusk to book a room, Tusk reads the dates and number of guests from your message on your device and shows an itemised receipt built from the property’s own listed rate and cancellation terms. RoamWise charges you nothing for this. Nothing is sent until you choose “Send request to the property”, which opens the stays page with the dates filled in. There you sign in, and the request (your name, contact number, dates, guests and any note you add) is shared with that property so it can reply. You pay only after the property confirms the room and total. Tusk never books or pays on its own, and it does not book flights, trains or buses; for those it only links you to the provider’s own site.

Ailon Tusk memory and feedback

Ailon Tusk learns your travel preferences from how you use it: home or starting city, usual budget and trip length, travel style, topics and places you ask about, and roughly when you plan. It does not store your messages, name, phone number or email in this memory. The summary lives only on your device. Ask Tusk “what do you remember about me” to see it, pause learning, forget one item or forget everything. If you choose “Sync to my account”, a copy of the summary is saved in your RoamWise account so it follows you to a new phone; RoamWise administrators can access account data, and turning sync off deletes the copy. If you tap the thumbs-down on an answer, the question text (with emails and long numbers removed), the kind of answer shown and the place name are sent to us without your account or device identifiers, kept for up to 30 days, and used only to fix wrong answers.

Trail Mesh nearby sharing (Android only)

Trail Mesh is optional and remains off until you review its disclosure, grant Android permissions and tap to start. It uses Google Play services Nearby Connections, Bluetooth and nearby Wi-Fi to communicate with verified nearby RoamWise devices. Your chosen trail display name is advertised locally while Trail Mesh runs. Text, team instructions, emergency alerts, voice notes and files you deliberately select are transferred device-to-device; RoamWise does not upload or retain those transfers on its servers. Recipients may retain or share content they receive.

If you create a field role, Trail Mesh stores your trail alias, role, expedition label, team code, voluntary check-ins, checkpoints, nearby roster state and incident notes locally on that phone. A team code is a grouping label, not a password. This information is shared only with devices you compare pairing digits with and accept; incident records stay local unless an authorised operator deliberately exports them. You can erase the local expedition data from the Trail Mesh privacy controls. Do not enter government IDs, phone numbers, medical diagnoses or unnecessary personal details.

Selected operator-trial and paid-operator eligibility is stored in your RoamWise account so the app can enforce the approved organisation and expiry. Users cannot grant or extend this access themselves. RoamWise does not use Trail Mesh for public traveller matching, follower feeds, contact-book uploads, advertising profiles or sale of field data.

The microphone is accessed only while you actively record a Pro voice note. Precise location is requested only when you choose to attach it to a Trail Mesh SOS. The Android file picker supplies only the file you select; RoamWise does not scan your media library. If a paid user explicitly enables multi-hop relay, lightweight messages and alerts may be forwarded through up to three verified devices; media and voice files are not relayed. You can stop discovery and advertising at any time. Trail Mesh is not satellite communication, does not guarantee delivery and does not replace local emergency services.

Google states that its Nearby SDK collects discovery/connection performance metrics and device model, country, build version and app package name to improve the API. Users control this through Android's Settings > Google > Usage & diagnostics setting. Google handles this information under its privacy terms.
