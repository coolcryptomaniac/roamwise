Day-wise Itinerary

Each day is split into morning, afternoon and evening operations with route intelligence, physical pacing and practical contingencies.

01

Haridwar Arrival - Begin at the Ganga

ROUTE: Delhi / Dehradun -> HaridwarDISTANCE: ~220 km from DelhiSTAY: HaridwarALT: ~314 m
MorningArrive by afternoon if possible and complete hotel check-in, pilgrimage document review and route briefing. Keep a printed and digital copy of the Yatra registration letter and ID.
AfternoonWalk the old lanes at an unhurried pace, visit a local temple if desired, and keep the first day light before the mountain drive begins.
EveningAttend Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri from a respectful viewing position. Return early for dinner and sleep.
Field intelThe official 2026 portal states Char Dham registration is compulsory and travelers should carry the registration certificate for verification.
02

Haridwar to Barkot - Into the Yamuna Valley

ROUTE: Haridwar -> Dehradun / Mussoorie corridor -> BarkotDISTANCE: ~180-220 km / 7-9 hrSTAY: BarkotALT: ~1,220 m
MorningDepart early to escape city traffic. Stop for breakfast on the way and move into the foothills with frequent controlled breaks.
AfternoonContinue through winding valleys toward Barkot. Check weather and the next morning's Janki Chatti access before dinner.
EveningEarly meal, pack a light trek bag, waterproof layer, walking pole, water, medication and darshan essentials.
Field intelMountain road times are elastic. Landslide management, traffic gates and pilgrim volume can add hours.
03

Yamunotri Darshan - The First Dham

ROUTE: Barkot -> Janki Chatti -> Yamunotri trek -> BarkotDISTANCE: Road + ~10-12 km round-trip trek depending startSTAY: BarkotALT: Temple ~3,293 m
MorningDrive pre-dawn or early morning to Janki Chatti. Begin the climb slowly. Ponies and palanquins may be available, but choose registered services and maintain patience on narrow sections.
AfternoonComplete darshan and descend before late-day mountain weather builds. Eat a warm meal after returning to the roadhead.
EveningReturn to Barkot, hydrate, stretch and rest. Keep the night completely free of optional sightseeing.
Field intelThe climb is steep and crowded during peak periods. Footwear with grip and an early start matter more than speed.
04

Barkot to Uttarkashi - Reset Between Dhams

ROUTE: Barkot -> Dharasu -> UttarkashiDISTANCE: ~80-100 km / 4-5 hrSTAY: UttarkashiALT: ~1,150 m
MorningA later start is reasonable after Yamunotri. Drive through the valley toward Uttarkashi and check in before lunch.
AfternoonVisit Vishwanath Temple or take a calm riverside walk. Repack for Gangotri and check the current traffic system on the narrow upper valley road.
EveningSimple dinner and early night. This day acts as a physical reset, especially for older travelers.
Field intelA good pilgrimage itinerary alternates strenuous days with easier transfer days; this reduces cumulative fatigue.
05

Gangotri - Bhagirathi Gorge and Glacier Country

ROUTE: Uttarkashi -> Harsil -> Gangotri -> Uttarkashi / HarsilDISTANCE: ~200 km round trip from UttarkashiSTAY: Uttarkashi or HarsilALT: Gangotri ~3,100 m
MorningLeave early via Harsil. Stop briefly in the valley for tea and acclimatization, then continue to Gangotri.
AfternoonDarshan and a gentle riverside walk near the Bhagirathi. Do not turn a temple day into an unplanned high-altitude trek.
EveningReturn before dark. If using Harsil as the night halt, enjoy the quieter valley and reduce driving; otherwise return to Uttarkashi.
Field intelWeather and traffic controls can change the timing of the Gangotri road. Treat official police and district instructions as authoritative.
06

Uttarkashi to Guptkashi - Long Himalayan Transfer

ROUTE: Uttarkashi -> Tehri / Srinagar -> Rudraprayag -> GuptkashiDISTANCE: ~220-260 km / 9-11 hrSTAY: GuptkashiALT: ~1,320 m
MorningStart before sunrise with packed breakfast. This is a long transfer across river confluences and mountain ridges.
AfternoonLunch around Srinagar or Rudraprayag depending traffic. Continue toward Guptkashi and avoid unnecessary roadside delays late in the day.
EveningCheck in, prepare Kedarnath daypack, confirm the next morning's transport to Sonprayag/Gaurikund and any slot or queue-token requirements.
Field intelThis is one of the itinerary's fatigue traps. A professional plan should include two drivers or regulated rest if the vehicle duty window is long.
07

Kedarnath Ascent - The Stone Temple Above the Valley

ROUTE: Guptkashi -> Sonprayag -> Gaurikund -> Kedarnath trekDISTANCE: Road transfer + ~16-18 km trek one waySTAY: KedarnathALT: ~3,583 m
MorningTransfer very early to Sonprayag, complete local transport formalities, and reach Gaurikund. Start walking in the cool hours with a slow sustainable rhythm.
AfternoonContinue the climb with regular water and food stops. Weather can change rapidly. Check into your Kedarnath accommodation before darshan rather than carrying all bags around.
EveningTemple darshan and, conditions permitting, a quiet evening view of the snow peaks. Keep exposure to cold controlled and sleep early.
Field intelHelicopter services are weather dependent and should never be the only plan. Book only through official/authorized channels and keep a road/trek contingency.
08

Kedarnath Dawn - Descend to Guptkashi

ROUTE: Kedarnath -> Gaurikund -> Sonprayag -> GuptkashiDISTANCE: ~16-18 km descent + roadSTAY: GuptkashiALT: Back to ~1,320 m
MorningWake before sunrise for a quiet temple atmosphere or early darshan if permitted. Start descending while legs are fresh.
AfternoonReach Gaurikund, transfer through Sonprayag and return to your hotel. Expect the descent to be hard on knees; walking poles help.
EveningHot meal, warm shower and complete rest. Do not schedule another sightseeing circuit.
Field intelKeep one light bag at Guptkashi and carry only essentials to Kedarnath; this materially improves trek comfort.
09

Guptkashi to Badrinath - Alaknanda Route

ROUTE: Guptkashi -> Chopta / Karnaprayag corridor -> Joshimath -> BadrinathDISTANCE: ~200-230 km / 8-10 hrSTAY: BadrinathALT: ~3,133 m
MorningDepart early and follow the officially open route toward Joshimath. Roadworks or controlled traffic windows may affect the exact path and timing.
AfternoonReach Badrinath before evening if possible, check in and dress warmly. Walk only gently after the long transfer.
EveningAttend darshan and absorb the dramatic Nar-Narayan mountain setting. Keep cameras respectful and follow temple photography rules.
Field intelAvoid building a minute-by-minute Badrinath arrival plan; buffer protects against mountain-road variability.
10

Badrinath and Mana - Then Down to Rudraprayag

ROUTE: Badrinath -> Mana (if open) -> Joshimath -> RudraprayagDISTANCE: ~160-190 km / 7-9 hrSTAY: Rudraprayag / SrinagarALT: Descend below ~1,000 m
MorningEarly darshan if desired, breakfast, then a short visit toward Mana only if road/access rules allow. Keep the visit brief because the return drive is long.
AfternoonDescend via Joshimath and the Alaknanda valley. Stop at a safe confluence viewpoint rather than rushing between every Panch Prayag.
EveningCheck in at Rudraprayag or Srinagar for a more relaxed final day. Celebrate completing all four Dhams with a calm dinner.
Field intelAfter multiple high-altitude days, descending and sleeping lower usually improves recovery.
11

Rudraprayag to Rishikesh / Haridwar - Close the Circuit

ROUTE: Rudraprayag -> Devprayag -> Rishikesh / HaridwarDISTANCE: ~140-170 km / 5-7 hrSTAY: Departure / optional RishikeshALT: ~340 m
MorningDrive downstream toward Devprayag and stop at a safe formal viewpoint to see the Bhagirathi-Alaknanda confluence.
AfternoonReach Rishikesh or Haridwar with enough time for onward rail, road or airport travel. If you have a late departure, use a day room rather than sitting exhausted in transit.
EveningTrip end. Keep one extra hotel night if connecting to an important international or long-haul flight the next morning.
Field intelA weather buffer after the Yatra is valuable during monsoon and shoulder-season travel.

Travel Intel

Operational notes to make the cinematic plan usable in the real world.

Registration

Uttarakhand Tourism's 2026 official portal states online Char Dham registration is open and compulsory; travelers should carry the registration certificate and complete verification at destinations.

Health

Warm layers, personal medication, rain protection, walking support and valid ID are essential. Elderly travelers and those with medical conditions should get individualized medical advice before strenuous high-altitude trekking.

Road reality

Do not treat map distance as normal highway timing. Weather, landslides, local traffic gates, parking controls and pilgrim volume frequently alter schedules.

Respect

This is a living pilgrimage, not a movie set. Keep noise, drone use and camera behavior within local and temple rules.
