Day-wise Itinerary

Each day is split into morning, afternoon and evening operations with route intelligence, physical pacing and practical contingencies.

01

Guwahati - Brahmaputra Gateway

ROUTE: Airport / station -> Guwahati cityDISTANCE: LocalSTAY: GuwahatiALT: ~55 m
MorningArrive and settle into a central hotel. Keep the first half-day flexible around flight timing.
AfternoonChoose one cultural anchor such as Assam State Museum or Kalakshetra, then take a relaxed city drive rather than overpacking the day.
EveningBrahmaputra riverfront at sunset. Brief the group on the trip's three different environments: warm Assam plains, wet Meghalaya hills, and cold high-altitude Arunachal.
Field intelPack modular layers; the temperature swing between Guwahati and Sela/Tawang can be large.
02

Kaziranga - Grasslands of the One-Horned Rhino

ROUTE: Guwahati -> KazirangaDISTANCE: ~210-240 km / 5-6 hrSTAY: KazirangaALT: Lowland
MorningDrive east after breakfast. Arrive around midday and check into a lodge near an operational park range.
AfternoonTake a regulated jeep safari if timing and park operations allow. Use a certified guide, remain inside designated routes, and keep wildlife distance.
EveningLocal cultural program or quiet lodge evening. Prep long lens, beanbag and neutral clothing for the next morning.
Field intelAssam Tourism lists November-April as the best general period for Kaziranga; operational ranges and safari availability are seasonal and can change.
03

Kaziranga Dawn - Then Shillong in the Clouds

ROUTE: Kaziranga safari -> ShillongDISTANCE: ~230-280 km / 6-8 hrSTAY: ShillongALT: ~1,525 m
MorningEarly safari in a different range if booked, then breakfast and checkout. Avoid chasing animals between zones; accept the randomness of wildlife viewing.
AfternoonDrive toward Shillong through rolling terrain. Stop at Umiam Lake if daylight and traffic allow.
EveningWalk Police Bazaar or a quieter cafe district, sample Khasi and Northeast cuisine, and sleep early.
Field intelKeep safari bookings and Meghalaya hotel check-in times flexible because park exit and road traffic can vary.
04

Shillong to Sohra - Waterfall Country

ROUTE: Shillong -> Mawkdok -> SohraDISTANCE: ~55-70 km plus local loopsSTAY: SohraALT: ~1,430 m
MorningVisit Shillong Peak or Elephant Falls early, then drive toward Sohra via Mawkdok-Dympep viewpoint.
AfternoonExplore Arwah or Mawsmai Cave and one major waterfall such as Nohkalikai, adapting to rain and visibility. Avoid rushing slippery trails.
EveningSunset attempt near Seven Sisters Falls or a cliff viewpoint if cloud cover opens. Warm dinner and an early trek briefing.
Field intelSohra changes character by season: monsoon gives maximum water but also low visibility and harder trail conditions; dry months often give clearer trekking weather.
05

Nongriat - Double-Decker Living Root Bridge Trek

ROUTE: Sohra -> Tyrna -> Nongriat -> SohraDISTANCE: Thousands of steps / full-day trekSTAY: SohraALT: Steep vertical descent/ascent
MorningStart very early from Tyrna. Descend at a measured pace through forest, suspension bridges and village paths toward Nongriat.
AfternoonSpend time at the Double-Decker Living Root Bridge and, only if fitness and daylight are sufficient, continue toward nearby pools or Rainbow Falls with a local guide.
EveningClimb back before dark, shower, rehydrate and do nothing else. The ascent is the hard part and can be punishing after rain.
Field intelThis day should be optional or modified for people with knee, cardiac or mobility limitations; there is no value in forcing it.
06

Dawki and Shnongpdeng - Crystal River Mission

ROUTE: Sohra -> Mawlynnong / Pynursla -> Dawki -> ShnongpdengDISTANCE: ~80-130 km depending routeSTAY: Shnongpdeng / ShillongALT: Low valley
MorningCheck the current Shillong-Dawki road advisory and choose the safer officially recommended route. Stop at a living-root bridge or Mawlynnong if it fits without turning the day into a bus tour.
AfternoonBoat the Umngot River with a licensed local operator when water and weather are safe. Continue to Shnongpdeng for quieter riverside time.
EveningRiverside sunset, then either camp/stay locally or return toward Shillong depending comfort and road status.
Field intelMeghalaya issued 2026 advisories after a Shillong-Dawki road disruption; route choice must follow current district guidance, not an old itinerary.
07

Return to Guwahati - Reset for the High Road

ROUTE: Shillong / Dawki sector -> GuwahatiDISTANCE: ~100-180 kmSTAY: GuwahatiALT: ~55 m
MorningSlow breakfast and drive back to Guwahati. Use this as a laundry, pharmacy and equipment-reset block.
AfternoonComplete Arunachal ILP checks, vehicle readiness and cash withdrawal. Verify the Bhalukpong-Dirang-Tawang weather corridor.
EveningEarly dinner. Pack cold-weather gear separately so it is reachable without unpacking the entire vehicle.
Field intelFor Indian citizens, Arunachal Tourism states tourist eILP is applicable for stays up to 14 days; always apply through the official system and carry ID/permit copies.
08

Guwahati to Dirang - Enter Arunachal

ROUTE: Guwahati -> Tezpur -> Bhalukpong -> Bomdila / DirangDISTANCE: ~320-360 km / 9-11 hrSTAY: DirangALT: ~1,500 m
MorningDepart before sunrise. Cross the Assam plains to the Bhalukpong entry corridor and keep permit/ID ready for checks.
AfternoonClimb through West Kameng toward Dirang. If traffic allows, stop at a roadside viewpoint or monastery, but preserve daylight for the mountain road.
EveningCheck into a homestay or hotel, warm meal, and rest. Dirang is a strategic acclimatization halt before Sela Pass and Tawang.
Field intelArunachal Tourism's official Tawang circuit runs Tezpur-Bhalukpong-Bomdila-Dirang-Tawang; a slower multi-day version is more comfortable than compressing it.
09

Dirang to Tawang - Cross Sela Pass

ROUTE: Dirang -> Sela Pass -> Jaswant Garh -> Jang -> TawangDISTANCE: ~135 km / 6-8 hrSTAY: TawangALT: Tawang ~3,000 m; Sela higher
MorningClimb toward Sela after checking snow, ice and traffic status. Dress in layers and keep photo stops short at high altitude.
AfternoonContinue via Jaswant Garh and Jang area, with a waterfall stop only if road parking is safe. Reach Tawang by late afternoon.
EveningHotel check-in, warm dinner and a gentle town walk only if energy is good. The body needs time after the rapid altitude gain.
Field intelSela Pass can be icy or snowbound outside peak summer. Local administration and road crews determine whether the route is safe.
10

Tawang - Monastery, War Memorial and Monpa Culture

ROUTE: Tawang local circuitDISTANCE: LocalSTAY: TawangALT: ~3,000 m
MorningVisit Tawang Monastery early when the courtyards are quieter. Move respectfully around prayer spaces and ask before photographing monks or ceremonies.
AfternoonExplore the craft center, local market, museum/war memorial and a Monpa cultural stop. Keep room for tea and slow conversation rather than only monuments.
EveningSunset over the valley and optional evening light show or memorial program if operating. Reconfirm next day's local high-altitude access.
Field intelThe official tourism circuit identifies Tawang Monastery and Sela Pass as core highlights; local military-zone access is separate and may need additional permissions.
11

High Lakes Day - Sangetsar and Optional Frontier Sector

ROUTE: Tawang -> high lakes sector -> TawangDISTANCE: Variable local high-altitude daySTAY: TawangALT: High plateau
MorningUse a locally authorized vehicle for the high-lakes circuit and carry any additional permits required. Sangetsar Lake is the scenic focus.
AfternoonIf the Bum La sector is officially open and your permits/vehicle qualify, it can be added; otherwise spend more time at accessible lakes and return early. Never pressure a driver or checkpoint for access.
EveningBack in Tawang before dark. Hot dinner, backup photos and rest.
Field intelFrontier permissions, road conditions and military restrictions can change at short notice. This day must be designed with a graceful Plan B.
12

Tawang to Dirang / Bomdila - Descend the High Road

ROUTE: Tawang -> Sela -> Dirang / BomdilaDISTANCE: ~135-180 km / 6-9 hrSTAY: Dirang / BomdilaALT: Descend to ~1,500-2,500 m
MorningLeave early and recross Sela in daylight. Stop only at safe pull-offs; mountain traffic can be slow and weather can close in quickly.
AfternoonArrive Dirang or Bomdila with time for a monastery, hot spring or short village walk depending energy.
EveningFinal mountain dinner and relaxed packing. The next day is a long exit to Guwahati.
Field intelSplitting the Tawang-Guwahati return over two days is far safer and more pleasant than a single endurance transfer.
13

Dirang to Guwahati - Expedition Exit

ROUTE: Dirang / Bomdila -> Bhalukpong -> Tezpur -> GuwahatiDISTANCE: ~320-360 km / 9-11 hrSTAY: Departure / GuwahatiALT: Back to plains
MorningDepart early and descend toward the Assam plains. Keep lunch flexible around traffic.
AfternoonContinue to Guwahati, arriving with a substantial buffer before any flight. Prefer a final hotel night if the next connection is important.
EveningMission complete: wildlife, cloud forests, living bridges, clear rivers and high Himalayan monasteries in one coherent arc.
Field intelFor commercial use, add one contingency night to protect against landslides, weather and road closures across three states.

Travel Intel

Operational notes to make the cinematic plan usable in the real world.

Arunachal permit

Arunachal Tourism says eILP is for Indian citizens and tourist eILP is applicable for stays up to 14 days. Foreign-national requirements differ; verify official rules before booking.

Meghalaya roads

Current advisories can reroute access to Dawki. Check Meghalaya Government / Tourism updates on the actual travel day.

Wildlife

Kaziranga safari operations are seasonal. Assam Tourism recommends roughly November-April for the park; ranges and rides are subject to official opening conditions.

Weather

Three-state travel creates huge weather variation. Keep waterproofs, warm layers, anti-slip footwear and a flexible road buffer.
