01 — The problem

Travellers don't lack inspiration.
They lack local knowledge.

Every AI trip planner produces the same output: a confident list of the same six places, written in the same voice, with "budget-friendly" where a number should be.

💸

No real prices

"Affordable" is not a number. Travellers get quoted 3× the local rate because they have no idea what the rate is.

🕳️

Confident hallucination

LLM planners routinely invent restaurants and recommend places that closed years ago.

📵

Useless offline

The moment you most need your itinerary is usually the moment you have no data.

🌍

Western-centric

Built for a traveller flying into Rome, not one taking a sleeper from Delhi.

02 — Insight

The gap is not intelligence.
It's ground truth.

Anyone can generate a Goa itinerary. Almost nobody can tell you that the auto to Anjuna should cost ₹180, that "your hotel is closed" is a commission scam, and then show you all of it with your phone in airplane mode.

Generic travel advice is now free and infinite. What remains scarce — and defensible — is verified local economic knowledge. That is a data moat, not a model moat, and it compounds.

03 — Product

What RoamWise does that others don't

🚕

Ground-cost engine

Auto ₹25–35 base +₹15–20/km. Bike taxi ₹10–15/km. Rooms, meals, intercity fares — bands for India, SE Asia and Europe.

🛡️

Anti-scam layer

The mechanics of each trick — meter refusal, commission runs, passport-as-deposit, dynamic currency conversion at the card machine.

📴

Offline-first

Saved trips, guides, maps and costs open with no connectivity. Survives a dead CDN.

🌱

Carbon ledger

Published DEFRA/IPCC factors, earned badges, shareable certificate — stating emissions avoided, never "removed".

📅

Crowd calendar

Month-by-month crowd index per destination. Timing changes trip quality more than any other decision.

💊

Situational safety

Pharmacies, rabies protocol, monkey hotspots in hill towns — the things that actually go wrong.

05 — Why the moat holds

A model is rentable.
Ground truth isn't.

They optimise for coverage

A global planner cannot afford to know that Rishikesh autos overcharge at Ram Jhula. RoamWise is worth more precisely because it goes narrow first.

Grounded, not generated

Answers are built from Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and curated cost models — so it can decline to answer instead of inventing a restaurant.

Costs scale with users, not with AI

Free data sources. No per-query inference bill. Gross margin does not degrade with growth.

Trust compounds

Every honest refusal — no fake urgency, no invented safety claims — is a reason a traveller returns. That is slow to build and hard to copy.
