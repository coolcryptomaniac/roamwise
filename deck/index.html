06 · Execution
Defensibility

Public data is the floor.
Verified local ground-truth is the moat.

Layer 1 — Public base (copyable)

OpenStreetMap, Wikivoyage, transit data. Anyone can use these. They give structure, not truth. On their own they cannot price a Rishikesh auto correctly.

Layer 2 — Verified ground-truth (ours)

Real cost bands, scam patterns and seasonal pricing, each checked by a paid contributor on the ground and stamped who + when. This does not exist online to scrape. We create it.

It compounds

Contributors re-verify on a cycle; users flag stale prices. A copyist starts from zero on launch day, and is a year behind by the time they match our base.

A giant won't do it

Optimising for 190 countries, no global player will pay to price-check autos in 40 Indian cities. The narrowness is the defensibility.

08 · Market
Business Model

Four revenue lines.
None of them a data sale.

Subscriptions

₹100 founder price → tiered subs and 3/5/10-year passes. Recurring, and higher for international users.

Affiliate

Flights, stays, activities. Never influences a recommendation — the cost engine computes identically either way.

Creator partnerships

Co-branded and sponsored itineraries. Revenue-share, so cost scales only when revenue does.

Merch + ads

Print-on-demand travel tees; website-only ads. Small, steady, zero-inventory.

No data sale. No paid placement. No dark patterns. The trust is the asset — we don't sell it.
