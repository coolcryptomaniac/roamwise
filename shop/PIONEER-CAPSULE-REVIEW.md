# RoamWise × Qikink: initial merchandising shortlist (research, NOT published stock)

**13 Nov 2026.** Market lenses: Myntra = style/fit and colour options; Flipkart = price-led discoverability and clear value communication; Amazon = exact titles, reliable size charts, high-quality real images, delivery/returns, verified buyer reviews. These are merchandising heuristics, **not an audited top-seller ranking** of all three marketplaces. Their private sales data is not available here.

Official Qikink catalogue states seller-order popularity: unisex tees, oversized tees, hoodies, tote bags, posters. Base catalogue pricing (before printing, GST, packing, shipping, failed delivery/RTO/returns) includes oversized standard tee US22 ₹225 and oversized classic UC22 ₹265, as of Qikink's published 2026 pricing. These are **not** net landed cost, so do not advertise a profit % before a live quote and sample.

**Avoid** selling untested water-resistant / mountaineering safety gear as protective equipment through a print-on-demand garment catalogue. Technical trekking accessories should use a separately vetted hardware supplier, actual specs and warranty.

## Production data and conversion gate
- Keep existing live shop items separate from concepts. New six-product capsule is **concepts only** until sampling and quotes are approved; no fabricated Qikink image, warehouse stock or checkout SKU.
- For each printed variant store actual Qikink SKU, fabric, colour, size, decorated artwork version, mockup plus real sample photos, tax rate, shipping region, price and RTO exposure. Mark `buyable=false` until SKU, supplier balance, size mapping and full payment order flow all pass.
- Require real delivered contribution after product+print+freight+tax+payment fee+returns/RTO+support. No discount is unconditional. Test two authentic visual directions, not copied anime characters, copyrighted art or false product ratings.
- Qikink confirms prepaid buyers pay RoamWise and Qikink deducts required fulfilment **credits**; it does not automatically sweep the consumer payment minus our margin into the supplier wallet. Do not auto-submit orders or promise dispatch on low wallet balance. Existing Firestore/KV and idempotency blockers remain.
