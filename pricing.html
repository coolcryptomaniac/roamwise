How billing works

  • Monthly and yearly tiers are recurring subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime; your access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Long-term and short-term passes, and the Founder offer, are one-time payments. There is nothing to cancel because there is no renewal.
  • A price change never affects something already bought. A lifetime pass stays lifetime. A long or short-term pass runs its full paid term at the price paid. An active subscription keeps its rate until its next renewal, and you will be told before any renewal changes.
  • Payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We never receive or store your card, UPI or bank credentials.
