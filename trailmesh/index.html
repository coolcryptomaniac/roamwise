An offline-capable mini app for preparing your next trek, keeping a local trip journal and sharing a safety briefing. Open once online to save it on your device.

Phone-to-phone Trail Mesh runs in the RoamWise Android app. It gives offline chat, SOS alarm, team roll call, verified pairing and file share over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with no mobile signal. Open it in the app or get the app. This kit works on any phone for planning, checklists, your journal and the safety briefing. Neither is satellite SOS or guaranteed rescue communication: carry standard navigation, an emergency kit and a dedicated emergency device where conditions need one.

Expedition plan

This plan is device-local, not synced to the operator. Do not store passwords, medical records or identity documents.

Pre-departure checks

Tick items only after independently confirming them. Share your intended route and return time with someone staying behind.

Safety essentials

  1. Check official weather, route closures, permits and avalanche conditions immediately before departure.
  2. Use a qualified guide for technical or unfamiliar terrain.
  3. Agree a turnaround time, regroup protocol and emergency fallback.
  4. No phone app can guarantee contact in valleys without network or compatible nearby devices.
