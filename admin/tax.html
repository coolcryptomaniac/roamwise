How this works

The entire ledger is read in pages rather than the staff console's 300-row preview. Confirmed live Cashfree order receipts are compared to matching ledger references. This is a diagnostic comparison, not gateway/bank settlement reconciliation. Revenue without references, unmatched paid orders, unsupported currencies and partner funds require review. No customer payment identifiers are included in the exported summary.

Direct payment capture, partner splitting and self-custody are deliberately outside this read-only workbench. Keep gateway credentials and wallet signing keys on separately controlled server infrastructure.
