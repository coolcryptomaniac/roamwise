# Founder finance desk — operational boundaries

- Cashfree bank change must be completed in the merchant dashboard and independently verified by the provider. Changing `paymentDestinations` in RoamWise only updates metadata; it cannot tell Cashfree which bank receives funds.
- The direct UPI VPA must belong to the new current account and be verified via a controlled test transaction and matching bank credit before publishing. Cashfree PG UPI is handled inside its own gateway settlement stream; manual UPI is a separate route.
- This tool imports CSV files locally in the browser, does not transmit files, store bank details, mutate the ledger, access banking APIs, pay anyone or file statutory forms. Import bank statement and NET Cashfree settlement export for the same period. Only exact UTR + net amount matches are marked reconciled; missing UTRs, refunds, fees and direct UPI credits need human evidence and review.
- Booking collections are not automatically platform revenue. Record recognised commission separately from payable vendor balances, GST/tax liabilities, refunds and investor capital. The bank balance is not company profit. Confirm accounting treatments with a CA.
- Keep bank portal credentials, OTP, tokens, PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements and personal employee details out of GitHub, ChatGPT prompts and client-side API keys. For continuous ingestion later, procure bank-supported read-only corporate account APIs or regulated Account Aggregator coverage and use server-side encryption plus least privilege; availability depends on the actual bank/account product.
- A private limited company may require a statutory auditor and company filings even at low revenue. Tax and marketplace GST obligations must be determined from entity, registered place, supplier/collector roles, transaction types and current law, not an unqualified ₹20 lakh trigger. No software should self-certify, submit or sign without required review and authorisation.
- Projections are sensitivity exercises on founder-entered assumptions, not a prediction or guaranteed VC commitment. VC funds are financing cash flow, not operating revenue.
