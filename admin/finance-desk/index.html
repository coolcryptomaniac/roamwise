Before switching your bank

Current-account cutover

RoamWise's Set active destination changes metadata only. It does not reconfigure Cashfree's actual settlement bank. Do not publish a new manual UPI ID until a small transfer is confirmed in the new account.
  1. Ask the bank to activate the business account, business UPI VPA/QR, statements and (if offered) authorised read-only business banking API access.
  2. Change and verify the Cashfree settlement bank in the merchant dashboard; retain the old settlement reports and note the effective cutover date.
  3. In Admin → Partner payments, add only a masked destination, record its verification, then update the manual UPI VPA and payee name to the bank-issued current-account details. Never put the full account number or login credentials into RoamWise.
  4. Make one controlled gateway payment and one direct UPI transfer. Confirm the new bank credits and settlement references; check refunds, reversals and old pending settlements before retiring the old route.
  5. Import a bank CSV and Cashfree net settlement CSV below. Match the same period. Direct UPI and expenses remain review items until supported by bank records and your sales/expense documents.

Founder assumptions · not a prediction

Revenue and operating-scenario desk

Enter your recognised annual RoamWise revenue, not gross hotel booking value, bank credits or a VC investment. No historical annual revenue has been verified, so all initial values are blank. Results are conditional calculations, not investor commitments or odds of fundraising.

Growth assumptions: cautious +35% annually in years 1–2, +20% in years 3–5, +10% thereafter; illustrative +90%, +40%, +15%; expansion +160%, +65%, +20%. Operating costs grow at your selected rate. Equity funding is excluded from revenue, profit and this forecast. A 10-year extrapolation has especially high uncertainty.

Fundraising is separate: ₹0 confirmed investment in this model until a signed financing instrument and bank credit are recorded. An expression of interest, pitch or hypothetical $500k–$5m raise is not cash or revenue.

Documents · human-reviewed drafts

Produce a professional handoff packet

Download a text packet of the reconciliation counts, model assumptions, document requests and unresolved questions for your accountant, lawyer or company secretary. It contains no raw bank narrations or private transaction rows and is not a tax return, invoice or certified financial statement.

What still needs a qualified person?

  • CA: incorporation/accounting setup, GST marketplace characterisation, TDS and any audit or returns requiring professional certification.
  • Company secretary/legal: company filings and board/share issues; review hotel contracts, guest refunds and privacy terms before signing.
  • HR/payroll: check the actual worker classification, state labour law, statutory deductions and personal-data handling before issuing employment documents.
