# Experiential property sourcing

The Property leads tab is an admin-only CRM for up to 1,000 targets, ranked into
a top-500 working queue. A score estimates partnership fit and approachability;
it is never a probability or claim that an owner will accept RoamWise.

## Evidence model

Every bundled suggestion has a source, source URL, evidence note and checked
date. The initial 41-property research set comes from public Responsible Tourism
Partnership award pages, Regenerative Travel profiles and Healing Hotels of the
World pages.

The list intentionally favors source quality over an arbitrary count. CSV
import expands the private CRM to 1,000 while preserving the same fields.
Before outreach, re-check the property, current reviews and official route.
Never guess an email pattern.

## Ranking

The 1–99 priority score weights experience fit, approachability, independent or
owner-led signals, a usable official contact route and written evidence. Actual
CRM replies can move a lead up; a decline moves it down. The UI explains every
score and keeps sending human-reviewed.

Recommended CSV columns:

`name,city,country,independent,tags,fit,approachability,reviewRating,reviewCount,reviewUrl,email,contactRoute,source,sourceUrl,sourceCheckedAt,evidence`

## Suggested operating cadence

1. Work 20–30 high-fit Indian independent properties first, especially Kumaon
   and nearby Himalayan operators.
2. Verify the owner or partnerships recipient from an official route.
3. Offer a small, reversible pilot with controlled room inventory.
4. Record response, follow-up date and objection in the CRM.
5. Move interested properties into the live Partner onboarding checklist.
