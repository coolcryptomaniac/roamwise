🛡️ Book with confidence

RoamWise can arrange a personalised travel package and offer a choice of full payment or an agreed advance after an itemised quotation and payment schedule are accepted. Your quote will separately show any RoamWise convenience/service fee, supplier charges and taxes before you pay.

If RoamWise cannot arrange the agreed package or service: you may accept an alternative or request a refund. If you choose a refund, RoamWise will initiate a refund of the amounts you paid for that unfulfilled booking, including the related convenience fee, within 7 calendar days after we confirm the service cannot be arranged. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to credit the money.

Customer-requested cancellations, partially delivered services and supplier-specific cancellation windows have separate terms disclosed in the individual quote before payment. Submitting an enquiry does not itself reserve a room or collect money.
