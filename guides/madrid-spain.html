The short answer: aim for April, May, December, skip August if you can, and read the packing notes before you book. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

What is the best time to visit Madrid?

April, May, December score highest on our weather-comfort index — daytime around 20.7°C with manageable rainfall. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

August is the toughest window (35.2°C days, ~16mm rain). Peak summer brings peak crowds and prices; late spring and early autumn are the insider windows.
