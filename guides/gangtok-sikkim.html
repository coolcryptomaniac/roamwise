Best Time to Visit Gangtok, India (Sikkim)

Quick answer: Go in June (early monsoon, clearer air) for the fewest crowds. Peak season is October–December. A budget week runs ₹5000–₹17000 depending on style. none (domestic, permits for Nathula/border areas).

Gangtok is known for MG Marg, Tsomgo Lake, gateway to Sikkim monasteries. This guide covers when to go, what it costs, and what to know before booking.

When to visit

The quietest window is June (early monsoon, clearer air) — expect noticeably thinner crowds than the October–December peak, though you should still check seasonal conditions (rain, road access, festival dates) before locking in dates.
