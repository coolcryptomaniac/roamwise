Best Time to Visit Kasol, India (Himachal Pradesh)

Quick answer: Go in March (before peak) for the fewest crowds. Peak season is May–June, September–October. A budget week runs ₹3000–₹10000 depending on style. none (domestic).

Kasol is known for Parvati Valley, Kheerganga trek, riverside cafés. This guide covers when to go, what it costs, and what to know before booking.

When to visit

The quietest window is March (before peak) — expect noticeably thinner crowds than the May–June, September–October peak, though you should still check seasonal conditions (rain, road access, festival dates) before locking in dates.
