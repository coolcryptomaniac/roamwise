Best Time to Visit Antalya: the Month-by-Month Truth

Antalya is a warm-all-year destination where rainfall, not temperature, decides your best window. The numbers below are real — monthly weather computed from the ERA5 climate archive for Antalya's exact coordinates — so this page answers the question most guides dodge: which months are actually worth your leave days?

The short answer: aim for March, April, October, skip July if you can, and read the packing notes before you book. The dry season's first and last weeks offer peak weather at below-peak prices.

🎒 What to pack for Antalya

Pack light cottons, reef-safe sunscreen, mosquito repellent, and one light rain layer. Comfortable walking shoes beat everything else in your bag.

🗓️ How to plan the days

Give Antalya 3–5 focused days. Mornings for the headline sights (crowds arrive after 10), afternoons for neighbourhoods, evenings for food. ❓ FAQ

What is the best time to visit Antalya?

March, April, October score highest on our weather-comfort index — daytime around 19.3°C with manageable rainfall. The dry season's first and last weeks offer peak weather at below-peak prices.

Which month should I avoid in Antalya?

July is the toughest window (37.7°C days, ~37mm rain). Humidity peaks with the rains — book air-conditioned stays in the wet months.

What should I pack for Antalya?

Pack light cottons, reef-safe sunscreen, mosquito repellent, and one light rain layer.
