The short answer: aim for April, July, November, skip January if you can, and read the packing notes before you book. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

Pack layers you can add or shed — the daily temperature swing is real. Comfortable walking shoes beat everything else in your bag.

Give Paris 3–5 focused days. Mornings for the headline sights (crowds arrive after 10), afternoons for neighbourhoods, evenings for food. ❓ FAQ

What is the best time to visit Paris?

April, July, November score highest on our weather-comfort index — daytime around 15.3°C with manageable rainfall. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

Which month should I avoid in Paris?

January is the toughest window (7°C days, ~67mm rain). Peak summer brings peak crowds and prices; late spring and early autumn are the insider windows.

What should I pack for Paris?

Pack layers you can add or shed — the daily temperature swing is real.
