The short answer: aim for June, August, December, skip September if you can, and read the packing notes before you book. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

September is the toughest window (19.1°C days, ~169mm rain). Peak summer brings peak crowds and prices; late spring and early autumn are the insider windows.
