Best Time to Visit Munnar, India (Kerala)

Quick answer: Go in June (monsoon greens, misty) for the fewest crowds. Peak season is October–December. A budget week runs ₹4000–₹15000 depending on style. none (domestic).

Munnar is known for Tea gardens, cool climate, Eravikulam wildlife. This guide covers when to go, what it costs, and what to know before booking.

When to visit

The quietest window is June (monsoon greens, misty) — expect noticeably thinner crowds than the October–December peak, though you should still check seasonal conditions (rain, road access, festival dates) before locking in dates.
