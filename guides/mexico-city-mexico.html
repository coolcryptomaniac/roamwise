Best Time to Visit Mexico City: the Month-by-Month Truth

Mexico City is a monsoon-rhythm destination — the year splits into a lush green season and a dry golden one. The numbers below are real — monthly weather computed from the ERA5 climate archive for Mexico City's exact coordinates — so this page answers the question most guides dodge: which months are actually worth your leave days?

The short answer: aim for January, February, March, skip July if you can, and read the packing notes before you book. Post-monsoon months are the secret: everything is green, waterfalls are full, and crowds haven't returned.

🎒 What to pack for Mexico City

Pack a compact umbrella or poncho, quick-dry layers, and waterproof covers for electronics. Footwear that survives wet streets earns its space twice over.

🗓️ How to plan the days

Give Mexico City 3–5 focused days. Mornings for the headline sights (crowds arrive after 10), afternoons for neighbourhoods, evenings for food. ❓ FAQ

What is the best time to visit Mexico City?

January, February, March score highest on our weather-comfort index — daytime around 23.8°C with manageable rainfall. Post-monsoon months are the secret: everything is green, waterfalls are full, and crowds haven't returned.

Which month should I avoid in Mexico City?

July is the toughest window (22.4°C days, ~316mm rain). Peak monsoon weeks can disrupt transport and close trails — build slack into plans.

What should I pack for Mexico City?

Pack a compact umbrella or poncho, quick-dry layers, and waterproof covers for electronics.
