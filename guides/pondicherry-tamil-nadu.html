Best Time to Visit Pondicherry, India (Tamil Nadu)

Quick answer: Go in June–July for the fewest crowds. Peak season is December–January. A budget week runs ₹3500–₹13000 depending on style. none (domestic).

Pondicherry is known for French Quarter, Auroville, beachfront cafés. This guide covers when to go, what it costs, and what to know before booking.

When to visit

The quietest window is June–July — expect noticeably thinner crowds than the December–January peak, though you should still check seasonal conditions (rain, road access, festival dates) before locking in dates.
