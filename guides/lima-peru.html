❓ FAQ

What is the best time to visit Lima?

January, March, April score highest on our weather-comfort index — daytime around 25.4°C with manageable rainfall. Autumn color or spring bloom weeks are short — time them and the trip upgrades itself.

Which month should I avoid in Lima?

December is the toughest window (22.3°C days, ~19mm rain). Peak summer brings peak crowds and prices; late spring and early autumn are the insider windows.
