    How tying in works

    Joining the rope is a written commitment on both sides, never just a logo on a page.

    1. Talk to Mohit

      A short call about where RoamWise is going and where you can help most.

    2. Read the terms

      Round terms are public on the invest page. Advisory equity comes from a fixed pool, set by contribution.

    3. Sign in writing

      An advisor agreement or investment documents, signed by both sides before anything is announced.

    4. Appear on the rope

      Your photo, bio and links go up only after signing, and only with your approval. The first meeting in Almora comes with a box of Bal Mithai.

    Rope rules

    The same rules apply to everyone on the rope, whoever they are.

    Only people who signed

    No "in talks", no borrowed logos, no famous names who once replied to an email.

    An honest update every month

    Users, revenue, what broke and what we learned, sent to everyone on the rope.

    Every knot is recorded

    The date each person joined is shown beside their name, and stays there.
