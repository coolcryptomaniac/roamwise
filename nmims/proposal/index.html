The idea, plainly

Most campus partnerships work like this: a brand pays for visibility, the fest gets a cheque, and everyone moves on once the banners come down. This proposal is built differently.

RoamWise gives 500 NMIMS students and organisers a real product they keep using long after the fest — lifetime access to an AI travel planner, at zero cost to the college and zero cost to the students. In return, RoamWise asks for the kind of visibility any campus partner would expect.

No cash changes hands in either direction. NMIMS also has the option to earn real commission afterward, if students beyond the 500 free passes choose to upgrade.
