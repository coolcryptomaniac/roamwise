MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

RoamWise × E-Cell NMIMS Mumbai — Barter Partnership

This document contains the binding terms. The Commercial Proposal explains the reasoning; this Annexure is what governs if the two ever disagree.

1. Parties and purpose

1.1 Parties

This MOU is between RoamWise, represented by its founder Mohit Pandey, and E-Cell NMIMS Mumbai or its authorised organising body, for a student-focused AI travel partnership around an agreed 2026 fest or activation.

1.2 Nature of consideration

The consideration is entirely in-kind (barter). Neither party owes the other a cash sponsorship fee for the scope described here. This does not affect Clause 6, a separate performance-based referral commission on additional paid sales.

2. RoamWise provides

2.1 Account allocation

Up to 500 lifetime RoamWise Pro activations, split 50 organiser + 450 student, claimed via a unique QR/link within the claim window (Clause 4).

2.2 Reference value

A founder-price equivalent of ₹50,000 (500 × ₹100) and an illustrative reference value of ₹1,50,000 (500 × ₹300). Context only — no cash obligation.

2.3 Campaign support

A claim mechanism, the Traveler DNA quiz concept, campaign creative/copy, and a post-campaign summary report.

3. NMIMS provides

3.1 Partner designation

Recognition as "Proposed AI Travel Partner," upgraded to "Official" automatically once this MOU is signed by both parties.

3.2 Category exclusivity

For the Event term only, no equal/higher "AI Travel Partner" designation to a directly competing product without RoamWise's written consent. Does not restrict airlines, hotels, OTAs, or non-competing brands.

3.3 Promotional support

Two collaborative reels, four stories, one LinkedIn post, two community pushes, one stage mention, a QR activation point, and help running the quiz/UGC challenge.

4. Claim window and eligibility

4.1 Window

Claims within 30 days after the Event. Unclaimed allocations revert to RoamWise.

4.2 Organiser verification

Organiser-tier claims require an invite code issued to NMIMS's core team, preventing outside claiming of that pool.

4.3 Persistence

Once activated, lifetime access continues under RoamWise's standard product terms regardless of this partnership's status.

5. Data handling

Aggregate campaign metrics may be shared. Person-level details only with explicit consent at registration. Passwords, payment credentials, and private trip content are never shared.

6. Optional paid-referral commission

6.1 Mechanism

One unique NMIMS referral code. Additional paid Pro purchases attributed to it earn NMIMS 30% commission on the tracked amount.

6.2 Exclusions

The 500 complimentary activations never earn commission. Refunds/chargebacks are excluded from reconciliation.

6.3 Settlement

Reconciled and paid after the campaign period, subject to standard invoice/tax documentation.

7. Missed deliverables

The remedy for any missed deliverable is a non-cash make-good of comparable value within 30 days — never a cash payment or penalty.

8. Term and termination

Begins on signature, concludes 30 days after the Event, except Clauses 4.3, 5, and 6 which survive. Either party may terminate for material breach after a written cure opportunity.

9. Entire agreement

This MOU and the accompanying Commercial Proposal (context only) form the complete understanding. Changes require written agreement by authorised representatives.
