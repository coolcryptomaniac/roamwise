01 / The proposed benefit

500 complimentary Founder Pro passes. Two protected pools.

RoamWise proposes an allocation of up to 500 lifetime Founder Pro activations for one agreed NMIMS/E-Cell fest or event campaign. Passes are for approved recipients only and are not available to anyone simply visiting this website.

50E-Cell core team & organisers

Reserved for organising-team members confirmed through E-Cell's authorised coordinator.

450NMIMS students & participating audience

For eligible students and approved participants in the agreed campaign, according to written eligibility criteria.

₹0Cash sponsorship

No cash sponsorship fee from either party for the proposed pass-for-promotion exchange.

Transparent value illustration: 500 × the ₹100 founder-offer reference is ₹50,000. A ₹300-per-pass comparison would equal ₹1,50,000; neither amount is money payable by NMIMS, an independently verified market value, or the current lifetime-Pro retail price.

03 / Partnership exchange

Deliverables, in writing, on both sides.

These are proposed obligations, not an accepted sponsorship or an official NMIMS endorsement. The final event dates, available channels, approval rights and any exclusivity must be authorised in writing.

RoamWise would provide

  • 500 lifetime Founder Pro passes, split 50 organiser / 450 eligible students and audience.
  • Private, verified, individually issued single-use codes and redemption support.
  • Co-branded promotional assets, QR instructions and a travel-personality quiz or agreed creative activity.
  • An aggregate post-campaign report on reach, passes issued and activated, and paid referrals where applicable.

E-Cell / NMIMS would provide

  • Campaign-specific AI travel-planning partner recognition, only when authorised.
  • 2 collaborative Reels, 4 Stories, 1 LinkedIn post and 2 campus/community pushes.
  • An event-stage mention and suitable logo/QR activation placement.
  • One promotion for the agreed travel quiz or student challenge, plus campaign-reach metrics available to the team.

Any category exclusivity would apply only to the agreed campaign content and duration—not all NMIMS activities, departments or student choices. Creative approval, delivery dates and reasonable make-good arrangements belong in the final agreement.

04 / Eligibility and activation

A personal pass, never a public coupon generator.

After institutional sign-off, E-Cell's authorised team would confirm eligible organiser and student/audience groups. RoamWise privately issues an individual code to each approved recipient; a public QR leads to official instructions, not automatic pass creation.

Organiser pool · 50

Approved E-Cell team members are nominated or verified by their authorised coordinator.

Student/audience pool · 450

NMIMS students and approved participants are checked against the agreed eligibility conditions and event window.

Unique, one-time entitlement

Each code is bound to the recipient's verified RoamWise login email and redeems once. The proposed redemption period is 30 days after individual issuance; activated lifetime access continues under the plan terms.

Privacy-first reporting

No public recipient list. RoamWise handles verification details privately, shares aggregate outcomes by default, and shares personal data only with a valid purpose, notice and applicable consent.

The NMIMS programme has not launched. The admin pass desk remains disabled until institutional approval, the agreed privacy process, and a real-account issuance and redemption check.

06 / Additional paid referrals

An optional 30% commission—separate from the free passes.

Qualifying paid RoamWise subscriptions

The proposal includes a 30% referral bonus on approved, attributable paid subscriptions generated through an agreed NMIMS referral code. Referrals can originate from organisers or participating students, but the E-Cell/institutional payee and any individual incentives must be agreed in writing.

No commission on complimentary passes

The 500 Founder Pro pass activations are free and generate no commission. Only one referral bonus can be credited for any qualifying purchase; refunds, chargebacks and invalid or duplicated sales do not qualify.

Referral identifier proposed: NMIMS2026. The parties must agree attribution, payment base, verified recipient, tax documentation, reconciliation and settlement timing. No public referral campaign is active under this proposal.

07 / Student participation

Let the campus tell its own travel stories.

The proposed quiz/creative activation can include travel films, photography, music, comedy, art or a short story around a dream three-day trip. Format, permissions, prizes and any public NMIMS branding require E-Cell's approval.

Independent opportunity · Campus Creator Circle

A separate invitation for makers, filmmakers and storytellers.

College creators can pitch RoamWise a collaboration independently of the NMIMS institutional partnership. A creator pitch neither reserves a pass nor creates a paid contract; fees, travel support, deliverables and usage rights are agreed separately in writing.
