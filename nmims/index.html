01 / The proposed offer

500 passes. Two clearly defined pools.

RoamWise proposes to fund up to 500 lifetime Founder Pro activations for this specific NMIMS/E-Cell collaboration, subject to a signed agreement, eligibility, actual availability and the agreed campaign period. These are not sold to participants or awarded merely for visiting this page.

50Organising team

Reserved for E-Cell core team members and fest organisers identified by authorised E-Cell leadership.

450Students and participating audience

For eligible NMIMS students and approved participants under the agreed fest distribution criteria.

₹0Cash sponsorship

RoamWise contributes the proposed passes in a barter arrangement; no cash sponsorship fee is requested or promised.

Offer value, transparently: 500 passes × the ₹100 founder-era offer reference = ₹50,000. At a ₹300-per-account illustrative reference, the comparable figure is ₹1,50,000. These are reference calculations, not cash payments, a guaranteed resale value, or the present retail price of lifetime Pro.

03 / The barter exchange

Clear commitments on both sides.

These are the terms RoamWise is proposing for discussion, not representations that NMIMS or E-Cell has accepted them.

RoamWise proposes to provide

  • 500 lifetime Founder Pro passes across the agreed 50/450 pools, without a cash sponsorship fee.
  • Founder-controlled, individual pass issuance with email verification, unique single-use codes and a mutually agreed eligibility process.
  • A co-branded information/QR point, campaign creative, social copy and a travel-personality quiz or equivalent activity.
  • Dedicated coordination during the campaign and a post-campaign summary of consented, aggregate activation and engagement results.

E-Cell / NMIMS would provide

  • Proposed “Official AI Travel Partner” designation after approval, with narrowly scoped category protection for this campaign only.
  • 2 collaborative Reels, 4 Stories, 1 LinkedIn post and 2 campus/community pushes.
  • A fest-stage mention, suitable logo/QR visibility and one physical or digital activation touchpoint.
  • One student quiz or creative challenge promotion, plus reasonable campaign performance reporting and privacy-compliant coordination.

No wider institutional endorsement is implied. Any partner title, exclusivity and publicity apply only after written authorisation and only to the agreed campaign—not unrelated NMIMS events, students, programmes or departments.

04 / Distribution and safeguards

A personal pass, not a publicly generated coupon.

Once the partnership and beneficiary criteria are approved, E-Cell and RoamWise would coordinate nominations/eligibility. A QR code can lead to official instructions, but it will not self-issue a lifetime pass.

01 · Organiser pool (50)

E-Cell's authorised lead provides the approved organiser list or another agreed secure eligibility method. RoamWise reviews each allocation before issuing a code.

02 · Student and audience pool (450)

Eligible students and participating audience members follow the confirmed event criteria and claim window. RoamWise verifies and approves each pass rather than allowing anyone to mint one.

03 · Individually issued code

The pass includes a readable name/serial section and an unpredictable suffix. The recipient email is held privately; recipients sign in using their verified matching email to redeem once.

04 · Cap, duplicates and reporting

Founder-only issuance prevents duplicate recipient emails and reserves a seat atomically against the 50/450 limits. Only the agreed minimum data and consented aggregate results are shared.

Activation gate: The current NMIMS programme is proposed and the pass issuer remains disabled. No student list is uploaded, no pass is issued, and no campaign starts until authorised sign-off, privacy review and a real-account redemption test.

06 / Optional paid referrals

30% referral bonus, on top of the 500 complimentary passes.

30%

For tracked event sales from organisers or students

Proposed bonus on qualifying, attributable paid RoamWise subscriptions generated through the agreed NMIMS event by the organising team or participating students, using approved unique referral links or codes. Complimentary pass activations earn no commission. Refunds, chargebacks and cancelled purchases are excluded or reversed.

₹0

Up-front exchange

No cash sponsorship is requested for the passes-for-promotion barter. The 30% bonus is additional to the free passes, not a fee paid by recipients or a condition of student eligibility.

Attribution and payout: Before launch, agree in writing who receives each bonus (E-Cell or the referring organiser/student), individual eligibility, tracking, qualifying products, settlement schedule and refund handling. NMIMS2026 is a proposed campaign-level identifier, not an active referral promotion. A paid sale earns at most one referral bonus; unattributed purchases and complimentary redemptions receive no commission.
