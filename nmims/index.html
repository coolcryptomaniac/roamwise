03 / The proposed exchange

Clear value for both sides.

These are proposed campaign deliverables for review. NMIMS/E-Cell has not yet approved an official partner designation or committed to the activities below.

RoamWise proposes

  • 500 lifetime Founder Pro passes across the 50/450 allocation.
  • Verified, individual single-use pass distribution and support.
  • Co-branded creative assets, QR instructions and a travel-personality activity.
  • A post-event summary of relevant, privacy-respecting campaign results.

E-Cell / NMIMS would provide

  • A campaign-specific AI travel-planning partner designation, subject to approval.
  • 2 collaborative Reels, 4 Stories, 1 LinkedIn post and 2 campus/community pushes.
  • An event-stage mention and suitable logo/QR activation point.
  • Promotion of one student quiz or creator challenge and reasonable campaign reporting.

Any partner title or category exclusivity would be limited to the agreed campaign materials and dates, not the broader institution or unrelated events.

04 / Secure activation

A personal pass for each recipient.

After authorised sign-off, the partners would confirm eligible organiser and student/audience groups. Approved participants receive privately issued codes bound to their verified email accounts.

Organiser allocation

Up to 50 passes for the confirmed core team and organisers, distributed following an agreed eligibility check.

Student and audience allocation

Up to 450 passes for verified NMIMS students and approved participants in the agreed event window.

Single-use, recipient-bound codes

Each approved recipient receives a unique code and redeems using their matching verified account. A public QR provides instructions, not a pass generator.

Privacy and clear reporting

No public recipient list. Sharing and reporting are limited to consented or appropriately aggregated campaign data.

The NMIMS programme has not launched. No public pass redemption is available; distribution starts only after formal approval and the agreed activation checks.

06 / Additional earning opportunity

30% referral bonus on top of free passes.

30%

Qualifying event sales

A proposed referral bonus on tracked, qualifying paid RoamWise subscriptions generated through the event by organisers or participating students. Approved unique referral links/codes identify each eligible sale.

₹0

Cost to pass recipients

Founder Pro passes under this proposed allocation are complimentary; bonus earnings are separate. Complimentary redemptions earn no commission.

Before launch, the partners will agree on attribution, whether each bonus belongs to the individual referrer or E-Cell, eligible purchases, reconciliation, refunds and payout timing. Each qualifying sale can receive only one referral bonus. Unattributed purchases receive no commission.
