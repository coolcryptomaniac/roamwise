03 / In-kind partnership exchange

Deliverables agreed on both sides.

These are proposed obligations, not an accepted sponsorship or an official NMIMS endorsement. Event dates, channels, approvals and any limited exclusivity require written authorisation.

RoamWise would provide

  • 500 lifetime Founder Pro passes: 50 organiser, 450 eligible students and participating audience.
  • Private, verified, individually issued, email-bound single-use codes and recipient support.
  • Co-branded promotional assets, QR instructions and a travel-personality quiz or agreed creative activity.
  • An aggregate report on campaign reach, issued and redeemed passes and qualifying paid referrals, if enabled.

E-Cell / NMIMS would provide

  • Campaign-specific AI travel-planning partner recognition, only once authorised.
  • 2 collaborative Reels, 4 Stories, 1 LinkedIn post and 2 campus/community pushes.
  • Event-stage mention and a suitable logo/QR activation point.
  • One student quiz/creative challenge promotion and available aggregate reach metrics.

Any category protection applies only to the specific campaign materials and period; it does not limit NMIMS events, departments or student choices outside it. Creative approvals and make-good arrangements belong in the signed schedule.

04 / Eligibility and activation

Personal passes, never a public coupon generator.

After written approval, E-Cell's authorised lead confirms eligible organisers and students/audience. RoamWise privately issues individual codes. A campaign QR will provide official instructions, not automatic pass allocation.

Organiser pool · 50

Authorised E-Cell coordinator nominates or verifies the team.

Student/audience pool · 450

Eligible students and approved participants follow the written campaign criteria.

One account, one pass

Each unique code is bound to the intended verified RoamWise login email, redeemed once and valid for 30 days after its individual issuance. Activated lifetime access continues under the plan terms.

Privacy-conscious reporting

No public recipient list; aggregate outcomes are shared by default. Personal data is disclosed only for a justified purpose with required notice and consent.

No NMIMS passes are issued from this page. The private admin pass desk stays disabled until approval, deployed hardened security rules, and a real-account redemption test.

06 / Optional paid referrals

30% on qualifying paid subscriptions—separate from free passes.

Agreed paid purchases only

Proposed 30% commission on genuine attributable paid RoamWise Pro subscriptions through approved identifier NMIMS2026. Referral activity may come from organisers or participating students, but the payee and any individual incentives must be agreed in writing.

No commission on free passes

The 500 complimentary pass activations earn no commission. Only one referral bonus can be credited per qualifying purchase; refunds, chargebacks, invalid and duplicated sales do not qualify.

The final agreement must set the commission base, verified recipient, attribution, taxes/invoices, refund treatment and settlement timing. No public referral campaign is currently active.
